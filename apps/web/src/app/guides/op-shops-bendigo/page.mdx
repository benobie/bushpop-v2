# Best Op Shops in Bendigo

Updated 2026. Bendigo's secondhand scene centres on a compact CBD with both the
major charity chains and a small independent vintage strip on View Street, then
spreads out through Golden Square, Kangaroo Flat, Eaglehawk, Long Gully,
Strathdale and California Gully. This is where to thrift across the city,
precinct by precinct, with addresses and a note on what each store does best.
Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Bendigo CBD

- **Vinnies Bendigo** (31 Chapel St, Bendigo). The flagship Bendigo store,
  stocking homewares, fashion, electrical goods and furniture. Best for general
  browsing and volume in one stop.
- **Salvos Stores Bendigo** (57 High St, Bendigo). The CBD's main Salvos outlet —
  clothing, bric-a-brac, books and furniture. Best for pairing with Vinnies
  Bendigo on the same CBD loop.
- **Red Cross Shop Bendigo** (293 Lyttleton Terrace, Bendigo). Open all seven
  days, selling new and pre-loved clothing, accessories and bric-a-brac. Best for
  a reliable any-day stop.
- **Storehouse Thrift** (17 Mitchell St, Bendigo). A locally-run thrift store open
  Sundays — one of very few Bendigo op shops trading that day. Best for a Sunday
  browse when most other shops are closed.
- **Bendigo Uniting Op Shop** (53 Williamson St, Bendigo). A 10-minute walk from
  the train station, specialising in fashion items and an eclectic mix of
  accessories, homewares and collectibles — deliberately no furniture or
  electrical goods. Best for a clothing-and-accessory-forward browse.
- **Red Dove Op Shop** (21 Forest St, Bendigo). A small parish-run op shop
  focused on clothing and smaller items, open weekday mornings only. Best for a
  quick weekday-morning browse rather than a planned visit.
- **The Vintage Collection Bendigo** (Shop 5, 20-34 Bath Lane, Bendigo).
  Self-described as Bendigo's favourite store for vintage 90s, Y2K and pre-loved
  clothing. Best for streetwear and branded vintage — Nike, Adidas, Carhartt,
  Ralph Lauren.
- **Charlotte O'Hara Vintage / Recycled Clothing** (195 View St, Bendigo). New and
  used quality clothing including designer labels and vintage pieces, all sizes,
  plus shoes and jewellery. Best for a designer-and-vintage mix in one small
  boutique.
- **Julie's Vintage Clothing Boutique** (111 View St, Bendigo). An independent
  vintage clothing boutique with an active social presence. Best for dedicated
  vintage and retro browsing on the View Street strip.

## Golden Square

- **UnitingCare Golden Square** (Cnr Panton & Laurel Streets, Golden Square). A
  straightforward suburban church-run op shop, cash only. Best for an
  uncomplicated everyday browse.
- **Bendigo Salvation Army Thrift Shop** (2b Thistle St, Golden Square). A
  Corps-run community thrift shop that specialises in women's clothing, alongside
  bric-a-brac, books and furniture. Best for women's fashion specifically.

## Kangaroo Flat

- **Vinnies Kangaroo Flat** (117-119 High St, Kangaroo Flat). A Vinnies store
  covering fashion, homewares, books, electrical and furniture. Best for a broad
  browse in Kangaroo Flat.
- **Salvos Stores Kangaroo Flat** (T4/301 High St, Kangaroo Flat). Clothing,
  bric-a-brac, books, toys and furniture. Best for pairing with Vinnies Kangaroo
  Flat on the same High Street strip.

## Eaglehawk and Ironbark

- **Vinnies Eaglehawk** (23 High St, Eaglehawk). Fashion, homewares, books,
  electrical and furniture. Best for an everyday browse in Eaglehawk.
- **Eaglehawk Salvos Thrift Shop** (2-6 Marong Rd, Ironbark). A community-focused
  Salvos shop with clothing, shoes, books, jewellery and furniture, and
  refreshments while you browse. Best for a friendly, community-connected op-shop
  visit.
- **Eaglehawk Op Shop** (6 Sailors Gully Rd, Eaglehawk). Jointly run by Eaglehawk,
  Long Gully and Marong Uniting Churches for Bendigo UnitingCare. Best for
  supporting three local congregations' outreach work in one shop.

## Long Gully

- **Salvos Stores Bendigo (Whip Court)** (9 Whip Court, Long Gully). Clothing,
  bric-a-brac, books, furniture and mattresses. Best for high-volume hunting away
  from the busier CBD store.
- **Lifeline Loddon Mallee Store** (2/8 Wood St, Long Gully). A purpose-built
  thrift store funding Lifeline's local crisis-support call centre. Best for
  op-shopping with a direct local crisis-support benefit.

## Strathdale and Kennington

- **Salvos Stores Strath Hill** (Shop 6, 98 Edwards Rd, Strathdale). Clothing,
  bric-a-brac, books, toys and CDs. Best for an everyday Strathdale browse.
- **MS Community Shop** (Shop 29, Strath Village Shopping Centre, 134 Condon St,
  Kennington). A recycled-clothing shop run for MS Plus. Best for supporting a
  smaller, less-common charity while still browsing quality clothing.
- **Victory Op Shop** (90 Edwards Rd, Kennington). A church-run pre-loved clothing
  and goods shop supporting local family and outreach programs, on the same
  shopping strip as the Salvos and MS shops. Best for a third stop on the same
  Edwards Road run.

## California Gully

- **UnitingCare California Gully** (324 Eaglehawk Rd, California Gully). A mix of
  brand-new stock and quality pre-loved items — clothing, household goods,
  furniture and tested electrical items. Best for an everyday browse in
  California Gully.

## What to expect

The CBD is where Bendigo's secondhand scene stands apart from a standard
regional-city op-shop run — View Street and Bath Lane carry three independent
vintage boutiques within easy walking distance of the major charity stores on
Chapel Street and High Street. Everywhere else follows the familiar Vinnies,
Salvos and Uniting pattern spread through the surrounding suburbs.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Pair the CBD vintage shops with the charity stores.** The Vintage Collection
  Bendigo, Charlotte O'Hara Vintage and Julie's Vintage Clothing Boutique are all
  a short walk from Vinnies Bendigo and Salvos Stores Bendigo.
- **Storehouse Thrift is your Sunday option.** Most Bendigo op shops close Sunday
  — this is one of the few open that day.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
