# Best Op Shops in Canberra

Updated 2026. Canberra's secondhand scene centres on the Fyshwick warehouse strip,
where charity superstores sit alongside designer consignment and a proper vintage
marketplace, with a newer wave of Y2K and streetwear resale opening around Lonsdale
Street in Braddon. This is where to thrift across the city, area by area, with
addresses and a note on what each store does best. Every shop below was checked for
2026.

## Fyshwick

Canberra's biggest concentration of secondhand shopping, all within a short drive of
each other.

- **Salvos Stores Fyshwick** (15 Mildura St, Fyshwick). One of Canberra's largest op
  shops, spread across two levels with furniture, clothing, homewares, books and
  electricals. Best for high-volume hunting across every category.
- **The Shed** (Building 4, 1 Dairy Rd, Fyshwick). A low-cost reuse store with a $5
  clothes and shoes rack and a free kids' section, founded in 2025 by a Green Shed
  veteran. Best for rock-bottom prices.
- **Designer Op Shop Emporium (DOSE)** (1 Lyell St, cnr Barrier St, Fyshwick). A
  collective of stallholders selling designer and vintage fashion, homewares and
  collectables. Best for a designer-vintage browse under one roof.
- **Material Pleasures** (3 Barrier St, Fyshwick). Designer consignment trading since
  1987, with labels like Country Road, Oroton, Bec & Bridge and Zimmermann moving
  through the racks. Best for established Australian designer labels.
- **Dirty Janes Canberra** (80b Collie St, Fyshwick). A large vintage and antiques
  marketplace with more than 90 stallholders and an on-site café. Not purely
  clothing, but a genuine drawcard for a vintage day out. Best for browsing fashion
  alongside furniture, jewellery and collectables.
- **Anglicare Discount Store** (8 Wiluna St, Fyshwick). A bargain, by-the-kilo
  secondhand clothing outlet, open weekday mornings. Best for the cheapest per-item
  price in Fyshwick.

## Belconnen

- **Vinnies Belconnen** (cnr Rae & Purdue St, Belconnen). A standard charity op shop
  in the north. Best for an everyday budget browse.
- **Salvos Stores Belconnen** (Units 3-4, 38-40 Weedon Close, Belconnen). A
  full-range charity store. Best for an all-purpose thrift stop in Belconnen.

## Braddon and Civic

Lonsdale Street has become Canberra's newest resale strip, with a fresh generation of
vintage and consignment stores opening alongside the older charity shops.

- **Canberra Garms** (G16/27 Lonsdale St, Braddon). Y2K and '90s vintage streetwear
  and archive designer pieces, opened in 2025 by five former Depop sellers who also
  buy clothes from the public. Best for Y2K and archive streetwear.
- **Goodbyes Braddon** (Unit 17/27 Lonsdale St, Braddon). The Canberra branch of the
  hybrid consignment chain, reselling Australian and international designer
  secondhand. Best for designer consignment on the same strip as Canberra Garms.
- **Vinnies Braddon** (3 Lonsdale St, Braddon). A charity op shop on the same
  strip. Best for charity-price basics between the newer resale stores.
- **Red Cross Shop Canberra City** (165 City Walk, Canberra City). A CBD charity
  store. Best for a lunchtime browse in the city centre.

## Inner South (Manuka)

- **Best Dressed Store** (ground floor, Endeavour House, 1 Franklin St, Manuka,
  opposite the Coles car park entry). A charity op shop run by Communities at Work,
  supplying free and low-cost interview, court and funeral-appropriate clothing to
  people in need. Best for smart-dress essentials with a genuine cause behind them.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at the Fyshwick charity superstores up to premium
for authenticated designer consignment at Material Pleasures and Goodbyes. Charity
shops restock through the week, and several of the Fyshwick and Braddon independents
keep specific opening days, so it is worth checking before a special trip.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Give Fyshwick a full morning.** The charity superstores, Dirty Janes and the
  designer consignment shops sit within a short drive of each other.
- **Go midweek** for fresh stock and quieter stores.
