# Best Op Shops in Brisbane

Updated 2026. Brisbane's secondhand scene runs from the vintage warehouses of West
End to the heritage Queenslanders of Paddington and a string of well-stocked charity
stores across the inner suburbs. This is where to thrift across the city, area by
area, with addresses and a note on what each store does best. Every shop below was
checked for 2026.

## West End and inner south

The densest cluster of vintage and charity stores in the city, walkable in an
afternoon.

- **Brik Market** (83a Vulture St, West End). A large upstairs vintage store that
  bills itself as Brisbane's biggest. Open Tuesday to Sunday. Best for Y2K, '90s
  streetwear and curated preloved.
- **ELC Vintage Clothing and Accessories** (46 Russell St, West End). A tiny room
  crammed with true vintage from the 1940s to the '90s. Open Friday and Saturday.
  Best for older, considered vintage pieces.
- **Yesterday's Thrift Shop** (235 Boundary St, West End). A budget charity store
  whose profits support a brain-injury charity, with six other Brisbane locations.
  Best for affordable everyday thrifting.
- **Vinnies West End** (381 Montague Rd, West End). A superstore-format charity shop
  of clothing, housewares and furniture. Best for a full-range budget browse.
- **Lifeline Shop West End** (148 Boundary Rd, West End). A charity store noted for
  retro collectables alongside the clothing racks. Best for a mixed dig.
- **Salvos Hope Op Shop** (2/7 Anthony St, West End). A charity store of clothing,
  furniture and bric-a-brac. Best for budget basics.

## Paddington and the inner west

Latrobe and Given Terraces hold the best vintage trail in Brisbane.

- **Empire Revival** (167 Latrobe Tce, Paddington). Rare vintage fashion alongside
  antiques and art, with more than 50 vendors inside the heritage 1929 Plaza
  Theatre. Best for a premium vintage and homewares experience.
- **Retro Metro** (297 Given Tce, Paddington). Women's and men's vintage, homewares
  and decor under a weathered Queenslander. Best for a relaxed vintage browse.
- **Barbarella's Vintage** (116 Latrobe Tce, Paddington). A basement-style shop of
  kitsch and collectable fashion, vinyl and toys. Open Thursday to Saturday. Best
  for retro curiosities.
- **Vinnies Paddington** (16 Latrobe Tce, Paddington). A curated charity store in a
  heritage-listed Foresters Hall. Best for a charity-price browse on the vintage
  trail.
- **Yesterday's Thrift Shop Paddington** (39 Nash St, Rosalie). A multi-room thrift
  store in an old worker's cottage. Best for a well-laid-out budget hunt.

## Fortitude Valley and New Farm

- **SWOP** (38 Wandoo St, Fortitude Valley). The buy, sell and trade chain's Brisbane
  store, relocated in 2025 to a warehouse space behind the James Street precinct.
  Open daily. Best for quality high-street, designer and curated vintage.
- **Australian Red Cross Shop** (Shop 245, McWhirters Centre, 298-312 Brunswick St,
  Fortitude Valley). A charity store of clothing and accessories. Best for a quick
  Valley browse.
- **Vinnies Fortitude Valley** (447 Wickham St, Fortitude Valley). A broad charity
  range of clothing and homewares; volunteer-dependent hours can vary, so call
  ahead. Best for budget basics.
- **Lifeline Shop Fortitude Valley** (693 Ann St, Fortitude Valley). A budget charity
  store with steady turnover. Best for low prices.

## CBD and southside

- **Lifeline Shop Brisbane City** (144 Adelaide St, Brisbane City). A central charity
  store. Best for a lunchtime thrift browse in the CBD.
- **Vintage Revival** (468 Ipswich Rd, Annerley). A Lifeline charity store with a
  deliberate vintage selection. Best for affordable clothing with a retro edit.
- **Salvos Stones Corner** (1/283 Logan Rd, Greenslopes). A large-format charity
  store on the Stones Corner op shop strip. Best for volume on the southside.

## Worth knowing: Lifeline Bookfest

Not a clothing store, but a Brisbane institution worth a mention. **Lifeline
Bookfest** runs twice a year at the Brisbane Convention and Exhibition Centre on
Glenelg Street, South Brisbane, with more than a million donated books plus games,
records and collectables. Entry is free and stock starts at a few dollars. Check
the Lifeline Queensland website for the next dates.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at charity stores up to premium for curated and
authenticated vintage. Charity shops restock through the week, and quality varies by
suburb and by week. Several of the independents keep limited hours, so it is worth
checking before a special trip.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Do the Paddington trail in one go.** The Latrobe and Given Terrace stores sit
  within an easy walk of each other.
- **Go midweek** for fresh stock and quieter stores.
