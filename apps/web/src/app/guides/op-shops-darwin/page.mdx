# Best Op Shops in Darwin

Updated 2026. Darwin's secondhand scene is compact but genuine: a small run of
independent vintage stores tucked into the CBD arcades, a solid band of Vinnies,
Salvos and Red Cross stores through Stuart Park, Nightcliff, Casuarina and
Palmerston, and a couple of specialist charity shops worth a special trip. This is
where to thrift across the city, area by area, with addresses and a note on what
each store does best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Darwin CBD

Smith Street Mall and the Cavenagh Street arcades hold Darwin's independent vintage
stores alongside two charity op shops, all within a few minutes' walk.

- **Vintage Twist** (Shop 5, Star Village Arcade, 32 Smith St, Darwin City). A
  boutique blending vintage pieces with contemporary fashion and locally made
  accessories. Best for a curated vintage-and-new mix in the Smith Street arcades.
- **Vintage Boutique Darwin** (Air Raid Arcade, 35 Cavenagh St, Darwin City). A
  vintage clothing store tucked into the Air Raid Arcade alongside a handful of
  other specialty shops. Best for a vintage dig while exploring the arcade.
- **Vinnies Darwin City** (5/48 Cavenagh St, Darwin). A standard charity op shop on
  the Cavenagh Street retail strip. Best for an everyday CBD browse.
- **Red Cross Darwin** (12/32 Smith St, Darwin). A Red Cross charity store on Smith
  Street Mall. Best for a quick city-centre stop between errands.

## Stuart Park and Fannie Bay

A short drive from the CBD, these two suburbs each hold one reliable charity op
shop.

- **Vinnies Stuart Park** (2 Westralia St, Stuart Park). A standard charity op
  shop. Best for an everyday browse close to the city.
- **Vinnies Fannie Bay** (Shop 1, 13 Fannie Bay Pl, Fannie Bay). A charity op shop
  stocking clothing, books and games. Best for a relaxed suburban dig near the
  museum and the Gardens.

## Nightcliff, Rapid Creek and Millner

Darwin's northern beachside suburbs have the city's best spread of charity and
community-run op shops.

- **Salvos Nightcliff** (48 Progress Dr, Nightcliff). A standard Salvos charity
  store on the Nightcliff shopping strip. Best for everyday basics.
- **Frilly's, Nightcliff Uniting Church Op Shop** (21 Cummins St, Rapid Creek). A
  church-run op shop open Friday afternoons and Saturday mornings, with a Saturday
  coffee stall alongside the racks. Best for a slower browse paired with a coffee.
- **Opportunity Knocks, Henbury School Op Shop** (2/62 Aralia St, Nightcliff). A
  charity op shop that doubles as a training placement for Henbury School students,
  running since 2009. Best for supporting a genuinely local cause while you browse.
- **Red Cross Jape Home Maker Centre** (Tenancy 10-12, 356-366 Bagot Rd, Millner).
  A Red Cross store inside the Jape Homemaker Village. Best for pairing a browse
  with a homemaker-centre shopping trip.

## Casuarina and Malak

Darwin's northern-suburbs retail hub, built around Casuarina Square.

- **Vinnies Casuarina** (3 Bradshaw Tce, Casuarina). A large-format charity store
  opposite Casuarina Square. Best for high-volume hunting in the northern suburbs.
- **Salvos Casuarina** (13 Scaturchio St, Casuarina). A Salvos store a short walk
  from Vinnies Casuarina. Best for pairing both stores on the same trip.
- **Red Cross Malak** (84 Malak Cres, Malak). A neighbourhood Red Cross op shop.
  Best for a quieter dig away from the Casuarina Square crowds.

## Palmerston

Darwin's satellite city has its own trio of charity op shops within a few minutes
of each other.

- **Vinnies Palmerston** (3 Rolyat St, Palmerston). A standard charity op shop
  serving Palmerston. Best for an everyday browse in the satellite city.
- **Salvos Palmerston** (C1/17 University Ave, Palmerston, in Palm Plaza). A Salvos
  store in Palm Plaza. Best for pairing with Vinnies Palmerston nearby.
- **Red Cross Palmerston** (32 Essington Ave, Gray). A Red Cross op shop in the
  Gray area of Palmerston. Best for rounding out a Palmerston op-shop loop.

## Winnellie and Berrimah

A light-industrial stretch of the Stuart Highway east of the city holds a cluster
of charity stores, including the RSPCA's.

- **Vinnies Winnellie** (468 Stuart Hwy, Winnellie). A charity op shop on the
  Stuart Highway. Best for a highway-strip stop.
- **Salvos Winnellie** (458 Stuart Hwy, Winnellie). A Salvos store a few doors from
  Vinnies on the same stretch of highway. Best for pairing both stores on one trip.
- **RSPCA Op Shop** (80 Boulter Rd, Berrimah). The RSPCA's Darwin thrift store,
  selling preloved clothing, books and homewares. Best for a charity browse that
  also supports animal welfare.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars for basics at the charity stores up to modest,
individually priced tags at the CBD's small vintage boutiques. Charity shops
restock through the week as donations come in, and several of the smaller church-
and school-run shops keep specific trading days rather than opening every day, so
it's worth checking before a special trip. Darwin's spread-out layout also means a
single day's thrifting usually means picking one or two precincts rather than
covering the whole city in one go.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Loop the CBD arcades first.** Vintage Twist (Star Village Arcade) and Vintage
  Boutique Darwin (Air Raid Arcade) are both a short walk from Vinnies Darwin City
  and Red Cross Darwin on Smith Street Mall and Cavenagh Street.
- **Check trading days before a special trip.** Frilly's at the Nightcliff Uniting
  Church and some of the smaller charity shops keep specific days rather than
  opening every day of the week.

  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
