# Best Op Shops in Wagga Wagga

Updated 2026. Wagga's op shops are heavily CBD-weighted: most of them sit within
a few blocks of Baylis and Fitzmaurice Streets, including a hospital auxiliary
shop and one run by Riding for the Disabled. Three Vinnies outlets spread the
load across the CBD, Turvey Park and Ashmont, and the most interesting
independent — Generocity — is out at Kooringal. This is where to thrift across
the city, precinct by precinct, with addresses and a note on what each store does
best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Wagga CBD

The Baylis and Fitzmaurice Street blocks carry most of the city's op shops.

- **Vinnies Wagga Wagga** (15 Peter St, Wagga Wagga). The Society's flagship Wagga
  store, carrying clothing, books, homewares and bric-a-brac. Best for the
  biggest single browse in the CBD.
- **Salvos Stores Wagga Wagga** (180 Forsyth St, Wagga Wagga). The Salvos family
  store, and the charity's only retail shop in town. Best for a full-category
  browse.
- **Australian Red Cross Shop Wagga Wagga** (121 Baylis St, Wagga Wagga). A
  standard charity op shop on the main street. Best for an everyday Baylis Street
  browse.
- **Riding for the Disabled Opportunity Shop** (41-43 Baylis St, Wagga Wagga). A
  niche charity shop whose proceeds fund the local RDA centre's equine therapy for
  people with disabilities. Best for a charity stop that funds something you
  won't find elsewhere.
- **Wagga Wagga Base Hospital Auxiliary Op Shop** (21 Baylis St, Wagga Wagga). A
  volunteer-run auxiliary shop, with proceeds buying equipment and services for
  Wagga Base Hospital. Best for a third stop on the same Baylis Street run.
- **Anglican Opportunity Shop** (23 Fitzmaurice St, Wagga Wagga). Run by the
  Anglican Parish of Wagga Wagga, selling clothing, household items and books.
  Best for a quieter browse a block off the main drag.
- **The Smith Family Store** (2/185 Morgan St, Wagga Wagga). A charity op shop
  funding The Smith Family's education programs for children. Best for an
  everyday browse near the CBD edge.
- **Vinnies John Gray Sorting & Distribution Centre** (5 Forge St, Wagga Wagga).
  An outlet attached to the Society's sorting and distribution warehouse, trading
  weekdays only. Best for seeing stock closer to the point it arrives, though
  it's a warehouse rather than a shopfront.

## Turvey Park

- **Vinnies Turvey Tops** (Shop 17, Turvey Tops Shopping Centre, Fernleigh Rd,
  Turvey Park). A standard charity op shop inside a neighbourhood shopping
  centre. Best for combining a browse with the weekly shop.

## Ashmont

- **Vinnies Ashmont** (Shop 7, Ashmont Mall, 51-53 Tobruk St, Ashmont). A standard
  charity op shop in Ashmont Mall, trading limited days. Best for a
  low-competition browse, but check it's open before you drive out.

## Kooringal

- **Generocity Opportunity Shop** (194-198 Lake Albert Rd, Kooringal). An
  independent, locally founded community op shop that puts its profits back into
  local community organisations rather than a national charity. Best for the
  unpredictable finds a chain's sorting process tends to filter out.

## What to expect

Wagga is a walk-the-CBD town rather than a drive-the-suburbs one. Six of the
shops above sit within a few blocks of each other, and three of them — the
hospital auxiliary, Riding for the Disabled, and the Anglican parish shop — are
small volunteer operations rather than national chains, which usually means
lower prices and less rigorous sorting. The outer suburbs are thin: Mount Austin,
Lake Albert and Forest Hill have no dedicated op shop we could verify, so Turvey
Park, Ashmont and Kooringal are the nearest options.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Walk Baylis Street.** Red Cross, the RDA shop and the hospital auxiliary shop
  are all on it, with the Anglican shop a block away on Fitzmaurice. Four stops
  on foot.
- **Favour the volunteer shops.** Small auxiliary and parish shops price by
  judgement rather than a chain's pricing matrix, which is where the mistakes in
  your favour happen.
- **Check Ashmont's days before driving.** It trades limited days, unlike the
  Peter Street and Turvey Park Vinnies.
