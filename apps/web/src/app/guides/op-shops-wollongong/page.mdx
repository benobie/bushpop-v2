# Best Op Shops in Wollongong

Updated 2026. Wollongong's secondhand scene centres on the CBD, where charity
stores, a social-enterprise thrift shop and a handful of curated vintage and
preloved boutiques all sit within a few blocks of Crown Street and its laneways,
walkable as a single trail. Fairy Meadow is the secondary stop, and a solid spread
of charity shops runs through Corrimal, Warrawong and Dapto. This is where to
thrift across the Illawarra, area by area, with addresses and a note on what each
store does best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Wollongong CBD and Crown Street

The Crown Street precinct is the densest run of secondhand shopping between Sydney
and the South Coast.

- **Vinnies Wollongong** (137-139 Crown St, Wollongong). A charity op shop right
  in the Crown St Mall, with mixed clothing, homewares and bric-a-brac. Best for
  an everyday charity browse in the middle of town.
- **Thrift Avenue** (1/233 Crown St, Wollongong). A social-enterprise thrift store
  selling affordable secondhand clothing, with community programs like free
  hygiene packs and interview-ready styling behind the racks. Best for budget
  finds that fund a local cause.
- **From Juni** (1 Crown Lane, Wollongong). Curated premium vintage and secondhand
  designer fashion with a sustainability focus. Best for designer and premium
  vintage.
- **The Classy Closet** (Shop 7B, 81-83 Church St, Wollongong). A vintage and
  contemporary preloved boutique that sources stock internationally. Best for
  affordable curated preloved a block off the mall.
- **Retro Groove** (4 Globe Lane, Wollongong). A laneway store filled with vintage
  fashion, homewares and oddities, from bucket hats to vinyl. Best for retro
  fashion and bric-a-brac in one dig.
- **Salvos Store Wollongong** (48 Kenny St, Wollongong). A standard charity op
  shop a short walk from the mall. Best for high-volume charity racks.
- **Lifeline Wollongong** (19 Ellen St, Wollongong). A charity op shop known for
  books as well as clothing. Best for a quiet browse off the main strip.

## North Wollongong

- **Vinnies Wollongong Clearance** (110 Montague St, North Wollongong). Vinnies'
  clearance outlet, open weekdays only, moving bulk discounted stock rather than
  curated racks. Best for bargain bulk hunting on a weekday.

## Fairy Meadow

The Princes Highway strip through Fairy Meadow is the second cluster, a short
drive or train hop north of the CBD.

- **Vinnies Fairy Meadow** (138-140 Princes Highway, Fairy Meadow). A standard
  charity op shop. Best for an everyday dig on the highway strip.
- **Salvos Store Fairy Meadow** (1/79 Princes Highway, Fairy Meadow). A standard
  charity op shop. Best for pairing with Vinnies on the same strip.

## Corrimal and Warrawong

- **Vinnies Corrimal** (67 Railway St, Corrimal). A standard charity op shop near
  Corrimal's shopping strip. Best for a suburban charity browse in the northern
  suburbs.
- **Vinnies Warrawong** (250 Cowper St, Warrawong). A standard charity op shop.
  Best for an all-purpose dig south of the city.

## Dapto

- **Lifeline Dapto** (55 Princes Highway, Dapto). A charity op shop on the main
  road through Dapto. Best for an everyday browse in the southern suburbs.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at the charity stores and the Vinnies clearance
outlet up to considered pricing at the curated boutiques around Crown Street's
laneways. Charity shops restock through the week, and the smaller independents
can keep limited opening days, so it is worth checking before a special trip.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Walk the Crown Street precinct in one go.** The CBD stores sit within a few
  minutes of each other on foot, laneways included.
- **Hit the clearance outlet on a weekday.** Vinnies Wollongong Clearance is
  closed weekends, and it is where the volume bargains are.
