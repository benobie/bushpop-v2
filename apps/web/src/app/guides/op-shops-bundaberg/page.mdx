# Best Op Shops in Bundaberg

Updated 2026. Bundaberg's op shops split three ways: the big national charities
in and around the CBD, a pair of superstore-format outlets facing each other on
Johanna Boulevard, and a genuinely strong set of local causes — a parish shop, an
animal-rescue warehouse, a homelessness charity, and a newer entrant that opened
its Svensson Heights store in late 2023. Bargara has its own Vinnies and Salvos,
so the coast isn't a drive back into town. This is where to thrift, precinct by
precinct, with addresses and a note on what each store does best. Every shop
below was checked for 2026.

## Bundaberg Central

- **Vinnies Bundaberg** (18 McLean St, Bundaberg). A standard charity op shop
  carrying clothing, bric-a-brac, manchester and books. Best for an everyday CBD
  browse.
- **Lifeline Shop Princess Street** (70 Princess St, Bundaberg Central). A
  clothing-focused charity op shop. Best for a quick clothes-only browse.
- **Dorcas Anglican Op Shop** (59 Woongarra St, Bundaberg Central). A parish-run
  shop beside Christ Church, funding Anglican community ministries. Best for a
  charity stop where the money stays local.
- **Angels Second 2 None** (67 Bourbong St, Bundaberg Central). Run by the Angels
  Community Group, which furnishes homes for people leaving homelessness and
  domestic violence. Best for a browse that directly funds housing people.

## Bundaberg South

- **Red Cross Shop Bundaberg** (Unit 4, Mid Town Shopping Centre, 9 Maryborough
  St, Bundaberg). A standard charity op shop selling new and donated clothing and
  homewares. Best for combining a browse with a shopping-centre trip.
- **RSPCA Op Shop Bundaberg** (12 Maryborough St, Bundaberg). An RSPCA Queensland
  shop, a few doors from the Red Cross. Best for pairing the two on one
  Maryborough Street stop.

## Norville and Kensington

Johanna Boulevard carries both of Bundaberg's superstore-format op shops.

- **Vinnies Johanna Boulevard** (68 Johanna Bvd, Bundaberg). Vinnies' first
  superstore-format shop in the region. Best for the single biggest secondhand
  haul in town.
- **Lifeline Superstore Bundaberg** (37 Johanna Bvd, Bundaberg). A larger-format,
  clothing-focused Lifeline that doesn't usually accept furniture. Best for
  high-volume clothes hunting, and for pairing with the Vinnies superstore
  opposite.

## Bundaberg West and Svensson Heights

- **Salvos Bundaberg Family Store** (1 Takalvan St, Bundaberg). A larger Salvos
  store covering furniture, clothing and homewares. Best for a full-category
  browse.
- **Neighbours Aid Op Shop Bundaberg** (7/1 Enterprise St, Svensson Heights).
  Opened in December 2023 as the charity's ninth and most northern store. Best
  for newer stock that hasn't been picked over by the regulars yet.

## North Bundaberg

- **Red Collar Rescue Op-Shop & Warehouse** (69 Perry St, North Bundaberg). An
  animal-rescue charity op shop and warehouse, with proceeds funding vet care for
  rescued animals. Best for a warehouse-scale dig, and a cause you can see the
  results of.

## Bargara

- **Vinnies Bargara** (Shops 1-3, 37 See St, Bargara). A standard charity op shop.
- **Salvos Bargara Family Store** (Beach Plaza, 15 See St, Bargara). A Salvos
  store a short walk from the Vinnies on the same street. Best for pairing the two
  on a beach day.

## What to expect

The national charities set the baseline — Vinnies, Salvos, Lifeline and Red Cross
each run a standard donated-goods store, and the two Johanna Boulevard
superstores are where the volume is. What makes Bundaberg worth a proper day is
the local layer around them: Dorcas at the Anglican parish, Angels Second 2 None
funding furniture for people exiting homelessness, Red Collar Rescue's
animal-welfare warehouse in North Bundaberg, and Neighbours Aid out at Svensson
Heights. Bargara's Vinnies and Salvos sit on the same street as each other, which
makes the coast an easy add-on rather than a separate trip.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Do both superstores in one stop.** Vinnies at 68 and Lifeline at 37 Johanna
  Boulevard face each other. Two of the biggest floors in the region, one car
  park.
- **Maryborough Street pairs up.** The Red Cross and RSPCA shops are a few doors
  apart.
- **Try the rescue warehouse.** Red Collar Rescue trades warehouse-style rather
  than boutique, which usually means less sorting and lower prices.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
