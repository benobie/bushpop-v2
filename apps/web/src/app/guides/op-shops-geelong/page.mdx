# Best Op Shops in Geelong

Updated 2026. Geelong's secondhand heart is Pakington Street in Geelong West,
where three big charity op shops and a designer consignment boutique sit within a
few blocks of each other on the same strip. Belmont's High Street adds a
back-to-back charity pair south of the river, with more spread through South
Geelong and Highton. This is where to thrift across the city, area by area, with
addresses and a note on what each store does best. Every shop below was checked
for 2026.

## Geelong West and Pakington Street

"Pako" is the strip that earns Geelong its op-shopping reputation, and it is
easily walked end to end.

- **Geelong West Uniting Op Shop** (23 Pakington St, Geelong West). A large-format
  op shop stocking clothing, homewares, books, music and toys. Best for a big
  all-rounder dig.
- **Salvos Store Geelong West** (4/23 Pakington St, Geelong West). A standard
  charity op shop in the same block as the Uniting store. Best for doubling up
  without moving the car.
- **Vinnies Geelong West** (53-55 Pakington St, Geelong West). A charity op shop
  with fashion and homewares. Best for budget basics on the strip.
- **Second Mode** (152 Pakington St, Geelong West). A consignment boutique
  specialising in preloved designer clothing and accessories, not a charity shop.
  Best for designer resale at Geelong prices.

## Belmont

High Street Belmont has a charity pair two doors apart.

- **Vinnies Belmont** (170 High St, Belmont). A standard charity op shop. Best for
  an everyday browse south of the Barwon.
- **Lifeline Op Shop Belmont** (174 High St, Belmont). A charity op shop right
  next to Vinnies. Best for pairing the two in one stop.

## South Geelong

- **Vinnies Geelong South** (162 Fyans St, South Geelong). A standard charity op
  shop. Best for a dig on the way through South Geelong.

## Highton

- **Highton Uniting Op Shop** (8 Porter Ave, Highton). A suburban charity op shop.
  Best for a quiet browse away from the strips.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at the charity stores up to boutique pricing at
Second Mode's designer racks. Charity shops restock through the week, so
mid-week visits often beat the weekend rush on Pakington Street. One to watch:
Victoria's first regional Savers superstore is slated to open in North Geelong's
"Big Red Shed" on Melbourne Road in late August 2026, which will add a
warehouse-scale option to the city.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Walk Pakington Street in one go.** The Geelong West stores sit within a few
  blocks of each other, with the Uniting and Salvos stores sharing a block.
- **Pair Belmont's two shops.** Vinnies and Lifeline are two doors apart on High
  Street, so one stop covers both.
