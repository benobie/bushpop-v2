# Vinted Australia

After years of Australians asking when they could use it, **Vinted launches in
Australia on 1 July 2026.** The European secondhand giant arrives with a national
Australia Post shipping partnership and a buying corridor into the United Kingdom.
Here is how it works, and how it stacks up against Bushpop and the other options
for buying and selling preloved fashion in Australia.

## Has Vinted launched in Australia?

Yes. Vinted goes live across Australia on **1 July 2026**, after a soft launch in
the weeks prior. Founded in Lithuania in 2008, Vinted is Europe's largest
secondhand fashion marketplace, and the Australian rollout is backed by a
multi-year Australia Post partnership that handles delivery nationwide. The launch
also opens a shopping corridor to the UK, so Australian members can browse and buy
from British wardrobes.

## How Vinted works in Australia

- **Free to sell.** Vinted charges sellers no listing or selling fees.
- **Buyer-paid fee.** Buyers pay a Buyer Protection fee on each purchase.
- **Integrated postage.** When an item sells, the app generates a prepaid Australia
  Post label; you drop the parcel at a post office or parcel locker.
- **App and web.** Create an account, list items with photos and a description,
  browse and buy directly in the app.

## Vinted vs Bushpop

Both Vinted and Bushpop are free to sell and buyer-funded, so the honest
differences come down to focus and what is on the platform today:

- **Bushpop is Australian-first.** It is built around Australian sellers, Australian
  brands and local sizing, with [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) for
  labels like Gorman, Country Road and Witchery that an international platform does
  not prioritise.
- **Bushpop has Australian inventory now.** Real preloved stock from Australian
  wardrobes, listed by locals.
- **Vinted brings scale and integrated shipping.** A huge global user base, the
  Australia Post label integration and access to UK listings.

There is no single right answer: many sellers will list on both. If you want a
local, fashion-focused marketplace with Australian brand guidance, Bushpop is built
for exactly that.

## Other ways to buy and sell secondhand in Australia

- **Depop** — strong for vintage and Gen Z fashion; available in Australia, with
  many international sellers.
- **Facebook Marketplace** — great for local pickup, but limited buyer protection
  and not fashion-focused.
- **eBay Australia** — huge inventory and solid buyer protection; less
  fashion-led discovery.
- **Gumtree** — local classifieds, best for pickup and bargains.

## Tips for buying secondhand in Australia

- **Know your measurements** and check a [size chart](/guides/size-charts/) before
  buying, especially for vintage and international brands.
- **Check seller ratings** and read recent reviews.
- **Ask about postage** and condition before you commit.
