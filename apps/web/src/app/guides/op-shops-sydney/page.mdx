# Best Op Shops in Sydney

Updated 2026. Sydney's secondhand scene spans everything from jam-packed charity
stores to curated vintage boutiques and high-volume thrift superstores. This is
where to thrift across the city, neighbourhood by neighbourhood, with addresses and
a note on what each store does best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Inner West

The heartland of Sydney thrifting, dense enough to do several stores on foot.

- **U-Turn Recycled Fashion** (2 Enmore Rd, Newtown). A long-standing buy, sell and
  trade favourite with a name-brand tilt and a dedicated designer rack. Best for
  '90s and Y2K vintage, Levi's, leather jackets and band tees.
- **Route 66** (Shop 2, 82-84 Enmore Rd, Newtown). A family-run institution trading
  since 1983. Best for Americana: cowboy boots, Western shirts, Levi's denim and
  leather.
- **Vinnies Glebe** (223 Glebe Point Rd, Glebe). A charity store that punches above
  its weight, with reviewers turning up the occasional designer piece. Best for
  budget general womenswear and menswear with a chance of a find.
- **Casablanca Marrickville** (330 Illawarra Rd, Marrickville). An immersive,
  family-run vintage store of clothing, art and furniture. Open Wednesday to Sunday.
  Best for considered vintage and collectibles.
- **Savers Camperdown** (93-99 Parramatta Rd, Camperdown). A three-storey thrift
  superstore restocked daily. Best for high-volume digging across every category at
  low prices.

## Eastern Suburbs

For the higher-end end of preloved, head east, where the donations skew designer.

- **U-Turn Recycled Fashion** (35 Hall St, Bondi Beach). The beachside outpost of
  the U-Turn chain. Best for preloved vintage, Y2K and name brands.
- **Vinnies Paddington** (292 Oxford St, Paddington). Stocked by the wardrobes of
  the Eastern Suburbs, so designer labels surface regularly. Best for a charity-price
  designer hunt, and worth going early on a weekday.
- **Wayside Chapel Op Shop** (29 Hughes St, Potts Point). A charity store where
  proceeds fund homelessness services, with a designer-to-vintage mix. Best for
  denim, knits and the occasional luxury piece with a good cause behind it.
- **Blue Spinach** (Redfern, by appointment). A consignment showroom for
  authenticated luxury, operating since 1996. Now appointment-only rather than
  walk-in, booked through their website. Best for verified designer handbags, shoes
  and womenswear.

## CBD, Surry Hills and Darlinghurst

The best single loop in the city for vintage and curated secondhand.

- **SWOP** (219 Oxford St, Darlinghurst). A boutique-feel store that buys and sells
  preloved designer pieces, paying sellers in cash or credit, so the racks stay
  curated and current. Open daily. Best for a seasonal designer edit.
- **Zoo Emporium** (180B Campbell St, Surry Hills). A 35-year vintage institution.
  Best for bold '60s, '70s and '80s statement pieces, tailoring and vintage heels.
- **C's Flashback** (314 Crown St, Surry Hills). American-sourced vintage with a
  second store in Paddington. Open seven days. Best for band and sports tees, '90s
  leather and denim.
- **Get Wasted Vintage** (546-552 George St, Sydney CBD). Vintage streetwear sourced
  from the USA, Japan and Korea. Best for Y2K, deadstock and denim.
- **Salvos Streetwear Store** (339 Crown St, Surry Hills, rear via Derby Lane). A
  charity store with a streetwear lens; proceeds fund Salvation Army programs. Best
  for budget secondhand fashion.

## North Shore and Northern Beaches

Quieter but rewarding, with well-kept coastal donations.

- **Vinnies Brookvale** (22 Roger St, Brookvale). Known for an unusually good mix of
  vintage, high-street and even luxury, reflecting Northern Beaches donations. Best
  for surf gear, sundresses and denim.
- **Op for Change** (41 Sydney Rd, Manly). A community store with a zero-waste model
  that donates all profits to local charities. Open daily. Best for all-round
  preloved clothing with a conscience.
- **Lifeline Manly** (46 Sydney Rd, Manly). Secondhand clothing with an entire floor
  given over to books; proceeds fund Lifeline crisis support. Best for a clothing
  and books browse in one stop.

## Western Sydney

Often the best value in the city, thanks to high turnover and low prices.

- **Savers Prestons** (Hoxton Park Central, 2 Lyn Parade, Prestons). A 1,600sqm
  thrift superstore with thousands of new items daily. Best for patient, high-volume
  treasure hunting.
- **Savers Marsden Park** (T1, 2 Ultimo Pl, Marsden Park). Western Sydney's newest
  large-format Savers, opened in 2025. Best for clothing across every category.
- **Savers Auburn** (315 Parramatta Rd, Auburn). A big-box thrift store of clothing,
  homewares and accessories. Best for volume at Western Sydney prices.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at charity stores up to premium for curated and
authenticated designer vintage. Charity shops tend to restock midweek after weekend
donations, so **Tuesday and Wednesday** often bring the freshest racks. Selection
varies a lot by location and by week, which is half the fun. Hours change, so it is
worth checking before a special trip, especially for the smaller independents.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Go midweek** for fresh stock and quieter stores.
- **Do a strip in one go.** Enmore Road in Newtown, Oxford Street through Paddington
  and Darlinghurst, and Crown Street in Surry Hills each reward a single walking
  loop.
- **Check the back racks** and the bins, not just the front displays.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
