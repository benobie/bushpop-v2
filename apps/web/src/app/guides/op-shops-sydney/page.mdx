# Best Op Shops in Sydney

Updated 2026. Sydney's secondhand scene spans everything from jam-packed charity
stores to curated vintage boutiques. Here is where to thrift across the city,
neighbourhood by neighbourhood, plus tips for finding the good stuff.

## Inner West

The heartland of Sydney thrifting. **U-Turn Recycled Fashion** in Newtown is a
long-standing favourite, with a name-brand tilt and a dedicated designer rack,
leaning into leather jackets, Levi's, band tees and '90s nostalgia. Around it,
Newtown, Marrickville and Glebe are dense with charity stores like Vinnies and
Salvos, all within walking distance of each other for a proper op-shop crawl.

## Eastern Suburbs

For the higher-end end of preloved, head east. **SWOP** in Darlinghurst buys and
sells preloved designer pieces, paying sellers in cash or store credit, so the
racks skew curated and current. **U-Turn** has stores at Bondi Beach and Bondi
Junction, and the area's Vinnies and other charity shops pick up quality donations
from the eastern suburbs.

## CBD and surrounds

Surry Hills sits right on the CBD's edge and is one of the best pockets in the city
for vintage and charity shopping in a single loop. Expect a mix of curated vintage
stores and charity shops carrying good-quality donations.

## North Shore

Quieter but rewarding. **Vinnies Brookvale** on the northern beaches is known for an
unusually good mix of vintage, high-street and even luxury, thanks to donations
that include surf-shop goods, sundresses and denim. The North Shore charity stores
generally see well-kept donations, so quality can be high.

## Western Sydney

Often the best value in the city. **Savers** runs thrift superstores at Prestons
(a 1,600sqm store restocked daily), Marsden Park and Auburn, where most items cost
under $10. **U-Turn** also has outlets at Campbelltown, Riverwood and Punchbowl.
High turnover plus low prices make the west worth the trip.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at charity stores up to premium for curated
designer vintage. Charity shops tend to restock midweek after weekend donations, so
**Tuesday and Wednesday** often bring the freshest racks. Selection varies a lot by
location and by week, which is half the fun.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Go midweek** for fresh stock and quieter stores.
- **Check the back racks** and the bins, not just the displays.
- **Know your measurements** — sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting room.
