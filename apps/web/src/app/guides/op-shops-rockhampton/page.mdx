# Best Op Shops in Rockhampton

Updated 2026. Rockhampton runs about a dozen op shops across the CBD, Allenstown,
Wandal, Park Avenue and the northern suburbs, with a handful more out on the
Capricorn Coast at Yeppoon and Emu Park. Vinnies alone accounts for four stores
in town. Beyond the national brands there's an Anglicare bargain store, an RSPCA
shop with cats on the floor, and an Endeavour Foundation store carrying a
pre-loved bridal section. This is where to thrift across the region, precinct by
precinct, with addresses and a note on what each store does best. Every shop
below was checked for 2026.

## Rockhampton CBD

- **Lifeline Superstore Rockhampton** (203 Campbell St, Rockhampton City). A
  larger-format Lifeline outlet. Best for high-volume hunting across every
  category.
- **Lifeline Warehouse Rockhampton** (236 Kent St, Rockhampton City). Part
  donation-sorting warehouse, part retail floor, rather than a tidy boutique
  shopfront. Best for digging through stock closer to the point it arrives.
- **Salvos Store Rockhampton** (60 William St, Rockhampton City). A standard
  charity op shop. Best for an everyday CBD browse.
- **RSPCA Op Shop Rockhampton** (39 Bridge St, Rockhampton City). An RSPCA
  Queensland shop that runs cat adoptions alongside the shop floor; well-behaved
  leashed dogs are welcome. Best for a charity stop that directly funds animal
  welfare.
- **AnglicareCQ Bargain Store** (193 Denison St, Rockhampton City). A
  volunteer-run Anglicare Central Queensland store that also sells bagged
  industrial rags alongside clothing, books and toys. Best for rock-bottom prices
  on everyday basics.

## The Range

- **Australian Red Cross Shop Rockhampton** (1 Canning St, The Range). A standard
  charity op shop, wheelchair accessible, just outside the CBD. Best for an
  everyday browse paired with the Campbell Street Lifeline.

## Allenstown and Wandal

- **Vinnies South Rockhampton** (67-69 Gladstone Rd, Allenstown). A standard
  charity op shop carrying clothing, manchester, books and toys. Best for the
  main southside browse.
- **Vinnies Wandal** (10 Wandal Rd, Wandal). A standard charity op shop. Best for
  a quieter browse away from the CBD.

## North Rockhampton, Berserker and Park Avenue

- **Vinnies Ford Street** (263 Ford St, North Rockhampton). A standard charity op
  shop. Best for an everyday northside browse.
- **Lifeline Shop North Rockhampton** (cnr Clifton St & Ford St, Berserker). A
  standard charity op shop a short walk from the Ford Street Vinnies. Best for
  pairing the two on one northside run.
- **Vinnies Park Avenue** (Shop 6, Park Ave Mall, 39 Main St, Park Avenue). A
  standard charity op shop inside a small suburban mall. Best for combining a
  browse with a regular shopping trip.
- **Endeavour Foundation Recycled Clothing** (299 Richardson Rd, North
  Rockhampton). Run by a disability-employment charity, stocking secondhand and
  some new clothing plus a pre-loved bridal section. Best for formal and bridal
  hunting, which most charity shops don't stock at all.
- **Salvos Stores Rockhampton North** (Shop 20, Red Hill Homemaker Centre, Yaamba
  Rd, Norman Gardens). A Salvos store inside a bulky-goods retail centre rather
  than a standalone shopfront. Best for a full-category browse on the northern
  run.

## Capricorn Coast

About 40km from the Rockhampton CBD, worth folding into a coast day rather than a
dedicated trip.

- **Vinnies Yeppoon** (74-76 John St, Yeppoon). A standard charity op shop. Best
  for a browse while you're on the coast.
- **Vinnies Emu Park** (25 Pattison St, Emu Park). A standard charity op shop in
  the smaller coastal town south of Yeppoon. Best for a quiet, low-competition
  browse.

## What to expect

Rockhampton is a national-charity-brand town. Vinnies, Salvos and Lifeline run
most of the floor space between them, and the shops are mixed clothing,
homewares and books rather than curated vintage or boutique secondhand. The
exceptions are worth knowing: Endeavour Foundation's bridal section at Richardson
Road, the RSPCA shop's adoptable cats on Bridge Street, and the Lifeline Kent
Street warehouse, which trades closer to a sorting floor than a shopfront. We
found no verifiable op shop in Gracemere.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Pair Ford Street with Clifton Street.** A Vinnies and a Lifeline sit within a
  short walk of each other in North Rockhampton and Berserker.
- **Go to Endeavour for formalwear.** A pre-loved bridal section is rare in
  charity retail, and it's the one category where secondhand savings are largest.
- **Try the warehouse.** Kent Street's Lifeline site doubles as a donation-sorting
  warehouse, which means less time between a donation landing and you seeing it.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
