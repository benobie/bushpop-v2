# Best Op Shops in Hobart

Updated 2026. Hobart punches well above its size, with a CBD consignment and vintage
cluster running from Elizabeth Street to Criterion Street, a proper retro strip in
North Hobart, and some of the best charity-shop digging in the state around Moonah
and Glenorchy. This is where to thrift across the city, area by area, with addresses
and a note on what each store does best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Hobart CBD

- **Hello Gorgeous Vintage** (Level 1, 147 Liverpool St, Hobart). A 25-year Hobart
  institution of curated vintage dresses, knits and coats, dry-cleaned and repaired
  before sale. Best for wear-ready vintage.
- **The Finders** (140 Harrington St, Hobart). A collaborative vintage store housing
  several stallholders under one roof, with a wide size and gender range and a $10
  rack. Best for browsing multiple vintage sellers in one visit.
- **Tommy Vintage Collective** (71 Murray St, Hobart, co-located with Sugarhill
  Vintage). '90s and 2000s streetwear, denim and band merch. Best for Y2K
  streetwear.
- **Bruce** (29 Criterion St, Hobart). Pre-loved designer consignment, with labels
  like Ganni, Frame, Max Mara, Issey Miyake and Comme des Garçons moving through.
  Best for a designer consignment hunt.
- **Goodbyes** (11-17 Argyle St, Hobart). High-end consignment resale with weekly
  colour-tag discounts. Best for consignment at a discount if you time your visit
  right.
- **Vinnies Hobart** (141-143 Elizabeth St, Hobart). A standard charity range of
  clothing, books and bric-a-brac. Best for a straightforward budget browse.
- **Salvos Stores Hobart** (11 Elizabeth St, Hobart). Clothing, homewares, books and
  a CDs and records bin, with click and collect. Best for an all-purpose op shop with
  a music-collector's bonus.
- **Lifeline Tasmania Hobart** (147 Argyle St, Hobart). Fashion, collectibles and
  homewares, with profits funding suicide-prevention services. Best for a charity dig
  with a strong cause behind it.
- **Australian Red Cross Shop Hobart** (123 Elizabeth St, Hobart). Clothing and
  smaller pre-loved items. Best for a compact CBD browse.

## North Hobart

The Elizabeth Street strip through North Hobart is Hobart's retro and vintage
heartland.

- **Vinnies North Hobart** (357 Elizabeth St, North Hobart). A store trading for more
  than 50 years, known locally for a strong retro furniture and collectibles range
  alongside the clothing racks. Best for retro homewares finds.
- **North Hobart Uniting Op Shop** (365 Elizabeth St, North Hobart). Fashion,
  homewares and collectables, open seven days with Friday hours until 9pm. Best for
  a thrift trip outside normal shopping hours.
- **Encore Store** (355 Elizabeth St, North Hobart). Curated women's pre-loved
  clothing alongside curios and garden and homewares. Best for a considered browse
  on the same strip.

## Moonah and Glenorchy

- **Vinnies Moonah** (98 Hopkins St, Moonah). Regularly cited by reviewers as one of
  the best op shops in Hobart, with a big furniture, clothing and homewares range.
  Best for a serious, unhurried dig.
- **Vinnies Glenorchy** (9 Cooper St, Glenorchy, opposite Chemist Warehouse). A
  standard charity range. Best for an everyday browse.
- **Salvos Stores Glenorchy** (396 Main Rd, Glenorchy). A standard Salvos range.
  Best for all-purpose secondhand shopping.
- **RSPCA Tasmania Hobart Adoption & Retail Centre** (55-57 Albert Rd, Moonah). Op
  shop stock funding animal welfare, open Tuesday to Saturday. Best for a charity
  browse with a soft spot for animals.
- **Hobart City Mission Moonah Mega-Store** (15 Main Rd, Moonah). The charity's
  flagship and largest store. Best for sheer volume in one stop.

## South Hobart

- **Vinnies South Hobart** (462 Macquarie St, South Hobart). A standard charity
  range. Best for a quieter southside browse.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at the charity stores up to premium for
authenticated designer consignment at Bruce and Goodbyes. Charity shops restock
through the week, and the smaller independents in the CBD and North Hobart can keep
specific opening days, so it is worth checking before a special trip.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Pair the CBD with North Hobart.** Elizabeth Street runs almost the whole way
  between the two, so a single trip can cover both.
- **Go midweek** for fresh stock and quieter stores.
