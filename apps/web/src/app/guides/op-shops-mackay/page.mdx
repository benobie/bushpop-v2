# Best Op Shops in Mackay

Updated 2026. Mackay punches well above its size for a regional city. All five
major charities trade in the CBD, Lifeline alone runs six branches from West
Mackay out to Sarina and Mirani, and a locally grown volunteer network — the
Kidney Support Network — runs four op shops of its own. Add an Endeavour store
with a pre-loved bridal section and a reuse shop attached to the Paget tip, and
there's a genuine day's thrifting here. This is where to shop, precinct by
precinct, with addresses and a note on what each store does best. Every shop
below was checked for 2026.

## Mackay CBD

- **Vinnies Mackay** (10 Macalister St, Mackay). A standard charity op shop with a
  large fashion department. Best for the single biggest clothing browse in the
  CBD.
- **Mackay Family Store** (48 Gregory St, Mackay). The Salvos family store. Best
  for a full-category browse across clothing and household goods.
- **Lifeline Shop Mackay** (287 Shakespeare St, Mackay). A standard charity op
  shop. Best for an everyday Shakespeare Street browse.
- **RSPCA Op Shop Mackay** (Shop 14, 129-139 Shakespeare St, Mackay, in the
  Gasworks precinct). The region's first RSPCA op shop, stocking pre-loved and
  vintage clothing, accessories and homewares, with proceeds funding the Mackay
  Animal Care Centre. Best for vintage, and the closest thing in town to a
  curated rack.
- **Australian Red Cross Shop Mackay** (76 Sydney St, Mackay). A standard charity
  op shop. Best for pairing with the Endeavour store two doors up the same
  street.
- **Endeavour Recycled Clothing Mackay** (137 Sydney St, Mackay). Run by the
  disability-employment charity Endeavour Foundation and trading for over twenty
  years, with secondhand and some new clothing plus a pre-loved bridal section.
  Best for formal and bridal hunting.
- **ADRA Op Shop Mackay** (83 Milton St, Mackay). Run by the Seventh-day Adventist
  charity ADRA, selling clothing, bric-a-brac, books and small electrical goods
  but no furniture. Best for a clothes-and-books browse without wading through
  furniture.

## North Mackay

- **Vinnies Mackay North** (2 Canberra St, North Mackay). A standard charity op
  shop. Best for an everyday northside browse.
- **Lifeline Shop North Mackay** (23 Creek St, North Mackay). A charity op shop
  that also stocks a "Vintage Revival" secondhand clothing range. Best for
  vintage north of the river.
- **Kidney Support Network Op Shop North Mackay** (2/45 Evans Ave, North Mackay).
  Entirely volunteer-run, with proceeds supporting local kidney patients. Best
  for a charity stop where the money stays in the region.

## South and West Mackay

- **Kidney Support Network Op Shop South Mackay** (1/237 Shakespeare St, Mackay).
  Volunteer-run, funding local kidney patients. Best for pairing with the
  Shakespeare Street Lifeline and RSPCA shops on one run.
- **Lifeline Shop West Mackay** (122 Nebo Rd, West Mackay). A standard charity op
  shop. Best for an everyday browse on the Nebo Road run.

## Mount Pleasant and Glenella

- **Lifeline Superstore Mackay** (7-14 Heaths Rd, Mackay). The larger-format
  Lifeline, well stocked with clothes and shoes. Best for high-volume hunting
  across every category.

## Andergrove and the Northern Beaches

- **Lifeline Shop Andergrove** (3/50 Oak St, Andergrove). A standard charity op
  shop, and the suburb's local drop point for secondhand clothing. Best for an
  everyday Andergrove browse.
- **Mackay Northern Beaches Family Store** (839 Mackay-Bucasia Rd, Bucasia). The
  Salvos store for the northern beaches. Best for a browse without driving into
  the CBD.

## Paget

- **Refindit by Outlook** (42 Crichtons Rd, Paget). A large reuse and
  recycled-goods shop attached to the Paget waste transfer station, run by the
  disability-inclusion social enterprise Outlook Australia since August 2025
  (formerly the Incredable Tip Shop). Furniture and bric-a-brac dominate, with a
  clothing section rather than a full op-shop rack. Best for homewares and
  furniture rather than a dedicated clothes hunt.

## Sarina, Marian and Mirani

- **Lifeline Shop Sarina** (24 Broad St, Sarina). A standard charity op shop.
- **Sarina Community Op Shop** (31 Broad St, Sarina). Volunteer-run through the
  Kidney Support Network, a few doors from the Lifeline. Best for pairing the two
  on a single Broad Street stop.
- **Kidney Support Network Marian Op Shop** (Daly St, Marian). Volunteer-run,
  funding local kidney patients.
- **Lifeline Shop Mirani** (Main St, Mirani). A standard charity op shop in the
  Pioneer Valley.

## What to expect

Mackay skews standard charity retail rather than curated vintage — clothing,
books and bric-a-brac, priced to move. The distinctive stops are the RSPCA shop
at Gasworks and Lifeline North Mackay's "Vintage Revival" range for anything
resembling vintage, Endeavour's bridal section for formalwear, and Refindit at
Paget for furniture. Worth knowing before you plan a trip: Trinity Op Shop, the
CBD's long-running independent favourite on Gordon Street, closed permanently in
March 2025 after losing its parish-hall lease, and the Vinnies at Sarina no
longer appears on the charity's own store locator.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Do Shakespeare Street as one run.** A Lifeline, the RSPCA shop and a Kidney
  Support Network shop all sit on it.
- **Sydney Street pairs up.** Red Cross at 76 and Endeavour at 137 are a short
  walk apart, and Endeavour is the only bridal rack in town.
- **Follow Lifeline out of town.** Six branches means six different donation
  catchments. Sarina and Mirani see far less competition than the CBD.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
