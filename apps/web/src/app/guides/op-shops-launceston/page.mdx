# Best Op Shops in Launceston

Updated 2026. Launceston's secondhand scene centres on a dense CBD stretch —
anchored by northern Tasmania's biggest Vinnies superstore — with independent
vintage boutiques on St John Street and Charles Street, then spreads out through
Invermay, Mowbray, Kings Meadows, Youngtown, Prospect and Ravenswood. This is
where to thrift across the city, precinct by precinct, with addresses and a note
on what each store does best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Launceston CBD

- **Vinnies Boland** (43A Boland St, Launceston). The flagship "Superstore" for
  northern Tasmania — over 700m² of floor space stocking roughly 30,000 clothing
  items at any time, plus furniture, books and music. Best for sheer volume and
  variety in one visit.
- **Salvos Stores Launceston** (22-24 Kings Way, Launceston). A central Salvos
  store stocking clothing, bric-a-brac, homewares, books and toys. Best for
  pairing with Vinnies Boland on the same CBD loop.
- **City Mission (Frederick Street)** (46-48 Frederick St, Launceston). The
  head-office shop of City Mission's northern Tasmania network, accepting and
  selling clothing, furniture, books and household goods. Best for an
  all-rounder charity browse in the CBD.
- **Red Cross Shop Launceston** (59A George St, Launceston). A central Red Cross
  op shop selling donated and some new clothing, accessories and homewares. Best
  for a solid all-round everyday browse.
- **HOPE Costumes & Thrift Store** (90 St John St, Launceston). A social
  enterprise of Teen Challenge Tasmania sitting between a traditional op shop and
  a vintage re-seller, with a fancy-dress and party twist and stock turning over
  almost daily. Best for costumes, party wear and quirky one-offs.
- **The ReDress Hub** (Quadrant Plaza, Shop 6&7/94 York St, Launceston). An
  independent, women-owned, LGBTQ+-friendly circular-clothing hub selling
  preloved and vintage clothing and running swaps, repairs and refashioning
  workshops. Best for vintage finds with a sustainability and community bent
  rather than a straight charity-shop browse.
- **Tassie Twos Vintage** (122-124 Charles St, Launceston). An independent vintage
  boutique specialising in hand-picked 80s-2000s, Y2K and retro streetwear.
  Best for curated Y2K and retro pieces rather than bulk browsing.

## Invermay

- **Salvos Stores Invermay** (157-161 Invermay Rd, Invermay). A larger-format
  Salvos store also stocking electrical goods and furniture alongside clothing.
  Best for a bigger single-visit haul than the CBD store.

## Mowbray, Newstead and Newnham

- **Red Cross Shop Mowbray** (403-407 Invermay Rd, Mowbray). A second Red Cross
  location serving the northern suburbs, the same donated-and-new clothing model
  as the George Street store. Best for pairing with the Invermay Salvos on the
  same run.
- **ADRA Connections Op Shop** (3/5 Joffre St, Mowbray). A tidy, volunteer-curated
  mix of clothing, books and homewares, wheelchair-friendly with dedicated
  parking. Best for a practical, low-price browse in Mowbray.
- **City Mission (Newnham)** (67a George Town Rd, Newnham). Part of the same
  network as the Frederick Street store. Best for general clothing and household
  bargains in the northern suburbs.

## Kings Meadows

- **Salvos Stores Kings Meadows** (89-93 Riseley St, Kings Meadows). A
  neighbourhood Salvos store — clothing, bric-a-brac, homewares, books and toys.
  Best for an everyday browse in Kings Meadows.
- **MS Plus Shop Kings Meadows** (145 Hobart Rd, Kings Meadows). Tasmania's first
  MS Plus community shop, funding multiple sclerosis support services. Best for
  supporting a specific cause behind every purchase.

## Youngtown

- **Encore Clothing (Self Help Workplace)** (414 Hobart Rd, Youngtown). Run by a
  not-for-profit Australian Disability Enterprise employing over 50 adults with
  disability — every garment is laundered and ironed, shoes disinfected, before
  sale. Best for genuinely clean, ready-to-wear secondhand clothing.
- **City Mission Youngtown** (351 Hobart Rd, Youngtown). A separate City Mission
  outlet a few doors from Encore Clothing, selling general clothing, furniture
  and kitchenware. Best for pairing with Encore Clothing on the same Hobart Road
  stop.

## Prospect

- **Prospect Mission Shop** (142 Westbury Rd, Prospect). A western-suburbs City
  Mission outlet — clothing, furniture, electrical and general household goods.
  Best for an everyday browse in Prospect.

## Ravenswood

- **Zions Hill Op Shop** (7-21 Warring St, Ravenswood). One of the largest op
  shops in Launceston by volume, focusing on clothing — summer and winter — and
  smaller items rather than furniture, wheelchair accessible. Best for a
  clothing-heavy rummage without wading through furniture.

## What to expect

Vinnies Boland is the standout — a 700m²-plus superstore that dwarfs every other
shop on this list and is worth building a trip around on its own. Beyond that,
City Mission runs the widest network across the city (Frederick Street, Newnham,
Youngtown, Prospect), so its stock varies most by location, while the CBD's three
independent shops — HOPE, The ReDress Hub and Tassie Twos Vintage — are where to
go for vintage and costume pieces you won't find in the charity rotation.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Start at Vinnies Boland.** Its 700m² floor and roughly 30,000 items in stock
  make it the biggest single haul in northern Tasmania.
- **Do the CBD vintage loop.** HOPE Costumes & Thrift, The ReDress Hub and Tassie
  Twos Vintage are all a short walk from each other and from Vinnies Boland.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
