# Best Op Shops in Ballarat

Updated 2026. Ballarat punches well above its size for secondhand shopping — a
dense CBD cluster around Bridge Mall, a well-known independent vintage strip on
Main Road in Bakery Hill, and charity shops stacked two and three deep through
Wendouree, Sebastopol, Alfredton and Delacombe. This is where to thrift across
the city, precinct by precinct, with addresses and a note on what each store does
best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Ballarat CBD and Bridge Mall

- **Vinnies Ballarat** (75 Bridge Mall, Ballarat). The flagship St Vincent de Paul
  store in the CBD, open long hours seven days. Best for a broad general browse
  right in the town centre.
- **Salvos Stores Ballarat** (12 Armstrong St North, Ballarat). Relocated in 2024
  to a larger site after 13 years in Bridge Mall, carrying clothing, bric-a-brac,
  books, toys, CDs/records and electrical goods. Best for volume and variety.
- **Red Cross Shop Ballarat** (406 Sturt St, Ballarat). A weekday-only secondhand
  clothing shop on the main Sturt Street strip. Best for quality secondhand
  garments at low prices.
- **Lifeline Ballarat** (128 Lydiard St North, Ballarat). An op shop attached to
  the Lifeline Ballarat centre. Best for op-shopping that directly funds
  crisis-support services.
- **Ballarat Hospice Care Op Shop** (723 Sturt St, Ballarat). A rebranded,
  boutique-style op shop funding in-home palliative care. Best for a more curated
  charity-shop browse.
- **BCC Thrift Shop** (204 Creswick Rd, Ballarat). A Salvation Army-partnered
  thrift shop where all funds stay local, selling bric-a-brac, clothing and some
  furniture. Best for a community-run alternative to the bigger charity chains.
- **Uniting Op Shop — Light Lounge** (107 Dana St, Ballarat). A traditional op shop
  inside Wesley Hall attached to a reading and rest area that funds a local meals
  program. Best for op-shopping with a direct community-welfare link.
- **Ballarat Bargain Browsers** (10 Eastwood St, Ballarat). Run by BRACE, offering
  affordable secondhand goods while training disadvantaged workers. Best for
  supporting local disability-employment training while you shop.
- **Rewind Ballarat** (42 Armstrong St North, Ballarat). A small independent shop
  specialising in 1960s-70s Australiana plus a dedicated vintage clothes rack.
  Best for retro homewares alongside a tight, well-curated clothing rack.
- **Reworn Market** (St Patrick's Hall, Lyons St South, Ballarat Central). A
  monthly quality-preloved-clothes market running since February 2022, where
  locals hire stalls to sell their own preloved clothing. Best for a rotating cast
  of individual local sellers rather than a fixed shopfront — check the next
  market date before visiting.

## Bakery Hill

Main Road in Bakery Hill is Ballarat's best-known vintage retail block, with
three independent shops within a few doors of each other.

- **Barbarona** (20 Main Rd, Bakery Hill, incl. Vintagy). Secondhand quality
  vintage and top-label clothing on Ballarat's main vintage strip. Best for a
  first stop on the Main Road vintage loop.
- **The Dressing Room Recycled** (20 Main Rd, Bakery Hill). A vintage, retro and
  recycled clothing specialist with a wide range of dresses. Best for dresses
  specifically, in the same building as Barbarona.
- **That Little Vintage Shop** (13 Main Rd, Bakery Hill). Vintage and designer
  clothing, shoes, handbags and accessories, sourced via the owners' own buying
  trips. Best for designer vintage pieces you won't find via a standard charity
  donation bin.

## Soldiers Hill

- **St John's Anglican Church Op Shop** (607 Lydiard St North, Soldiers Hill). A
  community op shop selling clothing, furniture and household items. Best for an
  everyday browse north of the CBD.

## Brown Hill

- **Brown Hill Uniting Church Op Shop** (Cnr Humffray St North & Thompson St,
  Brown Hill). A small church-run shop with most items under $10, funding a local
  learn-to-swim and school breakfast program. Best for rock-bottom prices with a
  direct local-community benefit.

## Invermay Park

- **BSS Op Shop (Browse - Shop - Support)** (800 Norman St, Invermay Park). An op
  shop run out of Ballarat Specialist School, staffed partly by students
  completing a retail VET certificate. Best for a shop where your purchase
  directly supports student work-experience training.

## Wendouree

- **Vinnies Wendouree** (1255 Howitt St, Wendouree). A St Vincent de Paul store on
  the Howitt Street strip, open seven days. Best for homewares, fashion and
  furniture in one visit.
- **Salvos Stores Wendouree** (1225 Howitt St, Wendouree). A full-size Salvos
  family store, separate from the Ballarat Central branch. Best for pairing with
  Vinnies Wendouree on the same Howitt Street run.
- **The Salvation Army Wendouree (Norman St Thrift Shop)** (1115 Norman St,
  Wendouree). A second, distinct Salvation Army-run outlet with change rooms and
  street parking. Best for a Salvos stop away from the busier Howitt Street strip.
- **Uniting Op Shop Wendouree ("Restore")** (1323 Howitt St, Wendouree). A
  notably long trading week covering furniture, fashion, shoes and accessories,
  plus handmade ceramics made by supported employees. Best for the longest
  opening hours of any Wendouree op shop.
- **St Matthew's Op Shop** (204 Forest St, Wendouree). Locally noted for selling
  designer clothing among its stock. Best for a designer find inside a standard
  charity shop.
- **Wozzle's Wearhouse — Wendouree** (1231A Howitt St, Wendouree). One of three
  Ballarat outlets of Wozzle's Wearhouse, a Child and Family Services charity
  selling fully-laundered quality preloved clothing. Best for clean,
  ready-to-wear secondhand clothing.
- **Ballarat Bargain Browsers — Wendouree** (2/1245 Howitt St, Wendouree). A
  second BRACE-run branch on the Howitt Street strip. Best for pairing with the
  other Howitt Street shops on one Wendouree trip.

## Sebastopol

Albert Street in Sebastopol carries six charity and community op shops within a
few blocks.

- **Vinnies Sebastopol** (112-114 Albert St, Sebastopol). A full-range Vinnies
  store open seven days. Best for a broad general browse in Sebastopol.
- **Sebastopol Salvos** (Cnr Beverin St & Grey St, Sebastopol). A Salvos family
  store serving the Sebastopol area. Best for volume alongside Vinnies Sebastopol.
- **Wozzle's Wearhouse — Sebastopol** (Shop 5, 58-60 Albert St, Sebastopol). A
  second CAFS-run Wozzle's outlet. Best for clean, laundered secondhand clothing
  on the Albert Street strip.
- **Rotary Op Shop** (2 Albert St, Sebastopol). A community "one-stop" op shop run
  by the Rotary Club of Ballarat West. Best for a broad stock range run by a
  well-known local service club.
- **Sebastopol Anglican Op Shop** (227 Albert St, Sebastopol). A small parish-run
  shop next to the bowling club. Best for a quick, low-key browse.
- **Carmel Op Shop** (263 Albert St, Sebastopol). A small church-run shop selling
  clothing, books, bric-a-brac and kitchenware, cash only. Best for an
  old-fashioned, low-price rummage.
- **Open Door Op Shop** (104-106 Albert St, Sebastopol). A Uniting Church-run shop
  in a repurposed former worship space. Best for an everyday Sebastopol browse.
- **Ballarat's Rainbow Rack** (206A Albert St, Sebastopol). Ballarat's first and
  only LGBTIQA+ op shop, everything new-to-near-new and nothing over $10, with
  member discounts for the LGBTIQA+ community. Best for gender-affirming wardrobe
  basics at very low, capped prices.

## Alfredton

- **Vinnies Alfredton** (1824 Sturt St, Alfredton). A Vinnies store on the Sturt
  Street approach into Ballarat. Best for a charity stop on the way into town from
  the west.

## Delacombe

- **Salvos Stores Delacombe** (307 Smythes Rd, Delacombe). A Salvos family store
  serving the growing Delacombe area. Best for high-volume hunting in the
  southwest suburbs.
- **Uniting Op Shop Delacombe** (899 La Trobe St, Delacombe). Notably long trading
  hours, open seven days. Best if you need an op shop open on a Sunday.

## Buninyong

- **Buninyong Lions Club Opportunity Shop** (206 Warrenheip St, Buninyong). A
  volunteer-run Lions Club shop serving this outer township, selling preloved
  clothing, homewares, books and bric-a-brac. Best for an op shop stop on a
  Buninyong day trip.

## What to expect

Ballarat's charity-shop density means a lot of doubling up — Vinnies, Salvos and
Wozzle's Wearhouse each run more than one branch across the city, so stock
between locations can differ a lot even under the same name. The real point of
difference is Bakery Hill's Main Road strip, where three independent vintage
shops sit within a few doors of each other, distinct from the standard charity
rotation everywhere else.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Do the Main Road vintage loop.** Barbarona, The Dressing Room Recycled and
  That Little Vintage Shop all sit within a few doors of each other in Bakery
  Hill.
- **Check the Reworn Market date.** It only runs monthly at St Patrick's Hall, so
  it's worth timing a CBD trip around it rather than assuming it's on.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
