# Best Op Shops in Gold Coast

Updated 2026. The Gold Coast's secondhand scene runs the length of the Gold Coast
Highway, from tourist-strip charity stores in Surfers Paradise and Broadbeach to a
run of distinctive independent vintage shops around Palm Beach and Coolangatta. This
guide covers permanent, regular-hours op shops rather than markets. For the
Coast's vintage market stalls, see our
[guide to the best vintage markets on the Gold Coast](/whats-on/the-best-vintage-markets-to-visit-on-the-gold-coast/).
Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Surfers Paradise and Broadbeach

- **RSPCA Op Shop Bundall** (116-118 Bundall Rd, Bundall, bordering Surfers
  Paradise). A boutique-feel charity store often flagged as one of the Gold Coast's
  best for furniture, alongside pre-loved fashion and homewares. Best for furniture
  finds alongside the clothing racks.
- **Red Cross Shop Surfers Paradise** (Shop 3, 3059 Surfers Paradise Blvd, Surfers
  Paradise). Curated seasonal clothing, shoes, accessories and homewares in a
  central tourist-strip spot. Best for a quick browse between the beach and the
  shops.
- **Old Soul Vintage** (2/2387 Gold Coast Hwy, Mermaid Beach). A small, owner-run,
  curated vintage clothing store sitting in a strip of retro shops on the highway.
  Best for a considered vintage edit.
- **Salvos Stores Mermaid Beach** (Shop 13, Pacific Square, 2532 Gold Coast Hwy,
  Mermaid Beach, bordering Broadbeach). A standard full-range Salvos store. Best for
  an all-purpose browse near the beach.

## Burleigh Heads, Currumbin and the south

- **Strange Days Store** (1/1142 Gold Coast Hwy, Palm Beach). Rock 'n' roll and
  Western-soul vintage plus upcycled clothing, with a second store now in Tweed
  Heads. Best for a distinctive vintage edit with real character.
- **Retrosheila** (17 McLean St, Coolangatta). Vintage clothing and collectables
  spanning the 1940s to the 1980s, plus retro party hire. Best for decades-spanning
  vintage near Coolangatta beach.
- **Lifeline Shop Burleigh Heads** (1722 Gold Coast Hwy, Burleigh Heads). A large,
  well-stocked general op shop on the main highway strip. Best for volume on the
  southern strip.
- **Lifeline Shop Currumbin Waters** (1 Stewart Rd, Currumbin Waters). A newly
  opened, bright and spacious store. Best for fresh stock in a newer fit-out.
- **Vinnies Palm Beach** (Shop 6/15 Palm Beach Ave, Palm Beach). A standard charity
  range. Best for an everyday browse.
- **Vinnies Coolangatta** (18B Griffith St, Coolangatta). A standard charity range.
  Best for a browse near the Tweed border.

## Southport, Nerang and the north

- **Salvos Stores Molendinar** (3-9 Precision Dr, Molendinar). The largest Salvos
  store on the Gold Coast, warehouse-scale and next to Bunnings. Best for deep stock
  at low prices.
- **Vinnies Southport** (20-22 Railway St, Southport). A standard charity range.
  Best for a CBD-adjacent browse.
- **Vinnies Nerang** (cnr Lavelle St and Nerang St, Nerang). A standard charity
  range. Best for an inland browse away from the beach strip.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at the charity chains up to considered pricing for
curated independents like Strange Days and Old Soul Vintage. Charity shops restock
through the week, and the strip along the Gold Coast Highway rewards a slow drive
rather than a single stop, since the best independents are spread from Mermaid Beach
down to Coolangatta.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Drive the highway strip.** Mermaid Beach, Palm Beach and Coolangatta each hold a
  worthwhile stop within a short drive of each other.
- **Go midweek** for fresh stock and quieter stores.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
