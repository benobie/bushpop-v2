# Best Op Shops in Sunshine Coast

Updated 2026. The Sunshine Coast's op-shop scene is spread across town centres rather
than one strip, from the charity-shop run down Aerodrome Road in Maroochydore to the
vintage and antique trail through Nambour's Howard and Bury streets, with further
clusters in Caloundra, Mooloolaba, Buderim and the Noosa hinterland. This is where to
thrift across the region, town by town, with addresses and a note on what each store
does best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Caloundra

Bulcock Street is Caloundra's retail spine and the base for most of the town's op
shops.

- **Vinnies Caloundra** (16 Mayes Avenue, Caloundra). A standard St Vincent de Paul
  charity op shop. Best for an everyday browse.
- **Red Cross Shop Caloundra** (Shop 3, 86 Bulcock Street, Caloundra). An Australian
  Red Cross charity store on the main Bulcock Street strip. Best for a stroll-in
  browse while shopping the strip.
- **Lifeline Shop Caloundra** (70/1 Bulcock Street, Caloundra). A Lifeline charity op
  shop, also on Bulcock Street. Best for pairing with the Red Cross shop on the same
  strip.
- **Salvos Caloundra Family Store** (5 Industrial Drive, Caloundra). The Salvation
  Army's Caloundra outlet. Best for high-volume hunting.
- **Bloomhill Op Shop Caloundra** (87A Bulcock Street, Caloundra). One of Bloomhill
  Cancer Care's nine Sunshine Coast op shops, funding cancer-care services for
  locals. Best for a charity browse that funds cancer support.
- **Neighbours Aid Caloundra** (35 Caloundra Road, Caloundra). A Neighbours Aid op
  shop that opened in 2009, raising funds for the charity's local and overseas
  community projects. Best for clothing, furniture and homewares in one stop.
- **Hospice Charity Shop Caloundra** (47 Caloundra Road, Caloundra West). A Mingary
  Care op shop funding Sunshine Coast palliative care. Best for a browse that
  supports local palliative care.

## Mooloolaba

Mooloolaba mixes independent vintage boutiques with a run of charity stores along
Brisbane Road.

- **Retournez-Vous** (Shop 3 & 4, 23 Brisbane Road, Mooloolaba). A secondhand and
  vintage boutique mixing consignment pieces with occasion wear. Best for
  eveningwear, races outfits and cocktail dresses.
- **Meraki Thread Co** (Shop 29, 123 Parkyn Parade, Mooloolaba). A hand-selected
  vintage and upcycled boutique at The Wharf Mooloolaba. Best for curated vintage
  without digging through racks.
- **Sanctuary Op Shop** (Shop 6, corner Walan and Smith streets, Mooloolaba). A
  charity op shop that puts its sales straight into Sonshine Sanctuary, a domestic
  and family violence crisis refuge near Woombye. Best for a browse that funds a
  local crisis service.
- **Helping Hand Charity Op Shop** (6 Meta Street, Mooloolaba). A Faith Church
  Sunshine Coast op shop. Best for an everyday budget browse.
- **Fusion Op Shop Mooloolaba** (Shop 7, 130-164 Brisbane Road, Mooloolaba). A Fusion
  Sunshine Coast op shop funding the charity's youth work. Best for affordable
  clothing and household goods.
- **Bloomhill Op Shop Mooloolaba** (Shop 1, 121A Brisbane Road, Mooloolaba). Another
  of Bloomhill Cancer Care's op shops. Best for a cancer-charity browse on Brisbane
  Road.

## Maroochydore and Cotton Tree

Aerodrome Road in Maroochydore carries the region's densest run of charity stores,
most of them within a few blocks of each other.

- **Salvos Maroochydore Family Store** (134 Aerodrome Road, Maroochydore). The
  area's Salvation Army outlet. Best for high-volume hunting.
- **Lifeline Shop Maroochydore** (88 Aerodrome Road, Maroochydore). A Lifeline
  charity op shop on the Aerodrome Road strip. Best for a reliable everyday browse.
- **Fusion Op Shop Maroochydore** (1/95 Aerodrome Road, opposite Aldi, Maroochydore).
  Fusion Sunshine Coast's flagship op shop, funding the charity's youth work. Best
  for affordable everyday clothing.
- **Katie Rose Cottage Hospice Op Shop** (Shop 7B, 54-56 Aerodrome Road,
  Maroochydore). A charity store funding Katie Rose Cottage's palliative care
  hospice in Doonan. Best for a browse that funds local hospice care.
- **Regain Hope** (48b Aerodrome Road, Maroochydore). A charity op shop run from a
  rear shed, raising funds to fight child abuse and human trafficking, stocked with
  everything from formal wear to vintage. Best for an unusual mix of formal wear and
  vintage finds.
- **Vinnies Maroochydore** (1 Norval Court, Maroochydore). A standard St Vincent de
  Paul charity op shop. Best for an everyday browse just off the main strip.
- **Little Sister's Coffee Co** (Shop 3, 96 Aerodrome Road, Maroochydore). A
  specialty coffee shop that also stocks a curated corner of secondhand and vintage
  clothing, including old Levi's and motorcycle tees. Best for combining a coffee
  stop with a vintage browse.
- **Fabulous Darling** (Shop 5, King Street, Cotton Tree). A vintage clothing store
  with an on-site alterations service, stocking preloved fashion from past decades.
  Best for vintage with alterations done on the spot.

## Buderim

Buderim's op shops sit along Main Street and Burnett Street in the village centre.

- **Bloomhill Op Shop** (41 Main Street, Buderim). Bloomhill Cancer Care's Buderim
  store, funding cancer-care services for Sunshine Coast locals. Best for furniture,
  clothing and homewares in one stop.
- **Fusion Op Shop Buderim** (13/43 Burnett Street, Buderim). A Fusion Sunshine
  Coast op shop funding the charity's youth work. Best for an everyday budget
  browse.
- **Beautiful You Cancer Charity Funky Op Shop** (82 Burnett Street, Buderim). A
  cancer-charity op shop built around a cafe meeting space, stocking preloved
  clothing, bric-a-brac and retro pieces. Best for a browse paired with a coffee.

## Nambour

Nambour's Howard and Currie streets carry a dedicated vintage-and-retro trail
alongside the usual charity run.

- **Mr Beesley's Vintage Clothing** (29A Howard Street, Nambour). A dedicated
  vintage clothing store for men and women, now based next to BackBeat Records
  after moving from the town's old ambulance station building. Best for authentic
  vintage clothing, shoes and accessories.
- **Saint Atlas** (10 Bury Street, Nambour). A vintage and antique furniture,
  homewares and clothing store. Best for pairing vintage clothing with vintage
  furniture under one roof.
- **Neighbours Aid Nambour** (99 Howard Street, Nambour). The founding store of
  Neighbours Aid, opened in 2002 and still raising funds for the charity's projects
  in Africa, India, Israel and Fiji. Best for a charity browse with real local
  history.
- **Vinnies Nambour** (14-22 Howard Street, Nambour). A standard St Vincent de Paul
  charity op shop. Best for an everyday browse on the Howard Street strip.
- **Helping Children Smile** (3/81-87 Currie Street, Nambour). A local charity op
  shop raising funds for Sunshine Coast children. Best for bargain prices on the
  Currie Street strip.
- **Salvos Nambour Family Store** (163 Currie Street, Nambour). The Salvation
  Army's Nambour outlet, with a dedicated vintage and collectibles section. Best for
  vintage finds inside a standard Salvos browse.
- **Lifeline Shop Nambour** (23 Lowe Street, Nambour). A Lifeline charity op shop
  known for its streetwear, manchester and women's labels. Best for streetwear and
  women's fashion labels.

## Cooroy and the hinterland

North-west of Nambour, the hinterland towns of Cooroy, Yandina and Eumundi each
carry their own thrift stop.

- **Bloomhill Op Shop Cooroy** (21 Maple Street, Cooroy). Another of Bloomhill
  Cancer Care's op shops. Best for a cancer-charity browse in the hinterland.
- **Waves of Kindness Cooroy** (11 Emerald Street, Cooroy). A Waves of Kindness
  charity op shop. Best for pairing with the Noosaville flagship on a hinterland
  loop.
- **Lifeline Shop Cooroy** (22A Maple Street, Cooroy). A Lifeline charity op shop.
  Best for an everyday browse in Cooroy.
- **Collective Haus** (19 Farrell Street, Yandina). A vintage furniture, clothing
  and coffee shop in Yandina. Best for vintage clothing alongside vintage furniture
  and a coffee stop.
- **Vinnies Eumundi** (Shop 2, 95 Memorial Avenue, Eumundi). A St Vincent de Paul
  charity op shop in Eumundi. Best for a thrift stop before or after the Eumundi
  Markets.

## Noosaville and Tewantin

Noosaville and neighbouring Tewantin hold the northern end of the Sunshine Coast
op-shop trail.

- **Waves of Kindness** (25 Production Street, Noosaville). Described by the charity
  itself as the Sunshine Coast's largest op shop, with a Care Centre attached
  offering community support. Best for the biggest single browse on the Coast.
- **Vinnies Noosaville** (4 Eenie Creek Road, Noosaville). A standard St Vincent de
  Paul charity op shop. Best for an everyday browse.
- **Salvos Noosa Family Store** (20 Eenie Creek Road, Noosaville). The Salvation
  Army's Noosa outlet. Best for high-volume hunting.
- **Katie Rose Cottage Hospice** (11a Bartlett Road, Noosaville). A furniture
  warehouse and community studio funding the hospice's palliative care. Best for
  furniture and homewares alongside clothing.
- **Endeavour Foundation Recycled Clothing** (1/97 Poinciana Avenue, Tewantin). A
  charity thrift store supporting people with disability, part of the Endeavour
  Foundation's national ERC chain. Best for clothing, manchester and bric-a-brac in
  Tewantin.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at the charity stores up to considered pricing at
the independent vintage boutiques in Mooloolaba, Cotton Tree and Nambour. Charity
shops restock through the week as donations come in, and the smaller independents
can keep specific trading days, so it is worth checking current hours before a
special trip. The same charity names (Bloomhill, Vinnies, Salvos, Lifeline, Waves of
Kindness) repeat across almost every town centre on the Coast, so if one location
doesn't have what you are after, the next town along usually will.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Work Aerodrome Road as one strip.** Most of Maroochydore's op shops sit within a
  few blocks of each other on the same road.
- **Treat Bulcock Street the same way in Caloundra.** The charity stores there
  cluster tightly enough to walk between them.
- **Make a hinterland loop.** Nambour, Cooroy, Yandina and Eumundi each have their
  own stop and sit a short drive apart.
