# Best Op Shops in Cairns

Updated 2026. Cairns spreads its secondhand scene along a few key corridors: the
Grafton Street and Lake Street run through the CBD, a cluster of charity stores
along Anderson Street in Manunda, and further outposts through the Showgrounds
precinct, Earlville, Smithfield and the northern beaches. This is where to thrift
across the city, area by area, with addresses and a note on what each store does
best. Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Cairns CBD

The Oceana Walk arcade off Grafton Street holds Cairns's two dedicated vintage
stores, a short walk from the CBD's charity shops.

- **Vintage Seeker** (Shop 11, Oceana Walk, 62 Grafton St, Cairns City). A vintage
  fashion boutique with a focus on 1970s pieces, accessories and menswear, plus
  in-house alterations. Best for 70s pieces and an outfit that needs tailoring on
  the spot.
- **Kaotica Vintage & Secondhand** (Shop 22-23, Oceana Walk, 62 Grafton St, Cairns
  City). A vintage and secondhand store in the same arcade, stocking vintage
  clothing, collectables and accessories. Best for a second vintage stop in the
  same arcade as Vintage Seeker.
- **Crackerbox Palace** (228 Sheridan St, Cairns North). A long-running vintage and
  antiques store with a dedicated room of vintage clothing alongside homewares,
  vinyl records and collectables. Open Wednesday to Saturday. Best for vintage
  clothing paired with genuine retro homewares and vinyl.
- **Vinnies Cairns CBD** (Shop 1, Marinos Arcade, 70 Grafton St, Cairns City). A
  standard charity op shop just off Grafton Street. Best for an everyday browse in
  the heart of the CBD.
- **Red Cross Shop** (50 Lake St, Cairns City). A charity store open seven days in
  the centre of the CBD. Best for a reliable daily browse.
- **Lifeline Shop Cairns Central** (211-215 Bunda St, Cairns City). A standard
  charity op shop close to Cairns Central. Best for budget basics on a Cairns
  Central shopping trip.

## Manunda and Westcourt

Anderson Street in Manunda carries two charity op shops on the one strip, backed up
by a Salvos family store and a Red Cross shop in the local shopping centre.

- **Vinnies Manunda** (82 Anderson St, Manunda). A standard charity op shop. Best
  for an everyday charity browse in Manunda.
- **Lifeline Shop Manunda** (159 Anderson St, Manunda). Another charity op shop on
  the same street as Vinnies Manunda. Best for pairing with Vinnies Manunda on one
  Anderson Street stop.
- **Salvos Cairns Family Store** (64 Hoare St, Manunda). The area's Salvos family
  store. Best for high-volume hunting across every category.
- **Red Cross Shop Raintrees** (Shop 9, Raintrees Shopping Centre, Koch St,
  Manunda). A Red Cross charity store inside the local shopping centre. Best for
  combining a browse with a regular shopping-centre trip.
- **Adventist Community Centre** (5 Bosanko St, Westcourt). A church-run charity
  store stocking men's, women's and children's clothing, footwear, accessories and
  books. Best for a lesser-known charity stop away from the main strips.

## Parramatta Park

- **The Preloved Project** (179 Bunda St, Parramatta Park, opposite Cairns
  Central). A secondhand and vintage market featuring emerging local designers,
  held periodically through the year plus occasional Saturday twilight markets.
  Best for a curated market day rather than a standing shopfront.

## The Showgrounds and Mulgrave Road

- **Vinnies Cairns Showgrounds** (157 Mulgrave Rd, Cairns). A standard charity op
  shop by the Showgrounds. Best for an easy stop alongside a Showgrounds visit.
- **Your Wardrobe Cairns** (Fred Moule Pavilion, Cairns Showgrounds, 132 Mulgrave
  Rd, Cairns). A preloved fashion market marking its 10th year in 2026, with over
  100 stallholders selling everyday bargains, designer labels and vintage pieces on
  its scheduled market dates. Best for the single biggest secondhand haul in the
  region, timed around its event dates.

## Earlville

- **Lifeline Shop Earlville** (471 Mulgrave Rd, Earlville). A standard charity op
  shop on Mulgrave Road. Best for an everyday browse in Earlville.
- **Animal Welfare Group Op Shop** (Telford St, Earlville). A profit-for-purpose
  charity store supporting local animal welfare. Best for a browse that supports
  animal rescue.

## Smithfield

- **Salvation Army Smithfield Family Store** (3-4/7 Salvado Drive, Smithfield). The
  Salvos family store serving the northern suburbs. Best for high-volume hunting
  close to home for Smithfield locals.
- **Lifeline Shop Smithfield** (Shop 2/7 Mt Koolmoon Drive, Smithfield). A charity
  op shop in Smithfield. Best for pairing with the Salvos store on the same trip.

## Stratford, Trinity Beach and the northern beaches

- **Vinnies Stratford** (1/9 Kamerunga Rd, Stratford). A standard charity op shop
  on the northern edge of the city. Best for a charity stop heading out of the
  CBD.
- **Vinnies Trinity Beach** (3 Coastwatcher Village, Cnr Trinity Beach Rd & Rabaul
  St, Trinity Beach). A charity op shop in the beachside village centre. Best for
  combining a browse with a Trinity Beach visit.
- **Marlin Coast Red Cross** (51 Trinity Beach Rd, Trinity Beach). A Red Cross
  charity store on the same strip. Best for a second stop right after Vinnies
  Trinity Beach.

## What to expect

Prices range from a few dollars at the charity stores up to considered pricing at
the independent vintage stores in the Oceana Walk arcade and at Crackerbox Palace.
Charity shops restock through the week, and the market-style stops — The Preloved
Project and Your Wardrobe Cairns — only run on set dates, so it is worth checking
before a special trip.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Do the Oceana Walk loop first.** Vintage Seeker and Kaotica sit in the same
  arcade off Grafton Street, a short walk from the CBD's charity stores on Bunda
  and Lake Streets.
- **Check market dates before you drive out.** The Preloved Project and Your
  Wardrobe Cairns are among the best hauls in the region but only run on scheduled
  dates through the year, not every week.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
