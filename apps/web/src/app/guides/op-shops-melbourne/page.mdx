# Best Op Shops in Melbourne

Updated 2026. Melbourne has one of Australia's strongest secondhand cultures, from
Brunswick charity shops to Fitzroy vintage boutiques. Here is where to thrift
across the city, area by area.

## Fitzroy and Collingwood

The Brunswick Street and Smith Street strips are prime thrifting territory.
**Hunter Gatherer** on Brunswick Street is a standout: a not-for-profit vintage
store run by the Brotherhood of St Laurence, known for bold prints, retro
silhouettes and colourful displays. The surrounding charity and vintage stores make
this an easy area to browse on foot.

## Brunswick

Sydney Road is wall-to-wall op shops. **Lost and Found Market**, an expansive
warehouse flea market on Lygon Street in Brunswick East, is a Melbourne favourite,
packed with vintage clothing, furniture and curiosities and restocked regularly.
**Salvos Brunswick** on Sydney Road offers a big, budget-friendly selection. The
sheer density here makes Brunswick the best suburb for volume hunting.

## CBD and surrounds

The **Nicholas Building** on Swanston Street is a vintage destination in its own
right. **RetroStar Vintage Clothing**, open since 2002 and one of Melbourne's
largest vintage stores, specialises in pieces from the 1940s through the 1990s, and
neighbouring stores like Vintage Sole add to the mix. The Queen Victoria Market
precinct is also worth a look for secondhand stalls.

## Northcote and Preston

High Street, Northcote, is quieter than the inner-north strips but sees strong
turnover through its charity stores, making it a good spot to find pieces others
have missed.

## South Yarra and Prahran

Chapel Street leans pricier and more curated. Expect boutique vintage and
consignment-style stores with a sharper edit, better for a considered find than a
bargain haul.

## Footscray

Often overlooked, the west delivers high turnover and low prices through its
charity stores. Worth the trip if you like digging for value.

## What to expect

Prices swing from a few dollars at charity shops to premium for curated designer
vintage. Stock turns over fastest midweek after weekend donations, and quality
varies by suburb and by week, so repeat visits pay off.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Shop midweek** for fresh stock and fewer crowds.
- **Do a strip in one go** — Brunswick Street, Sydney Road and Chapel Street each
  reward a single walking loop.
- **Know your measurements** — vintage sizing differs from modern labels. Our
  [size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when you are buying without a fitting
  room.
