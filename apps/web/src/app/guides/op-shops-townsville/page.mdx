# Best Op Shops in Townsville

Updated 2026. Townsville's secondhand scene is spread wide: a small CBD cluster
on Flinders Street, Australia's biggest Vinnies store out at West End, a run of
charity shops along the Charters Towers Road strip through Hermit Park and Hyde
Park, and further outposts through Garbutt, Aitkenvale, Kirwan, Deeragun and even
across the water on Magnetic Island. This is where to thrift across the city,
precinct by precinct, with addresses and a note on what each store does best.
Every shop below was checked for 2026.

## Townsville CBD

- **Vinnies Flinders Street** (271 Flinders St, Townsville City). A standard
  charity op shop in the heart of the CBD, open seven days. Best for an everyday
  CBD browse.
- **Red Cross Shop Townsville** (277 Flinders St, Townsville City). A charity
  store trading Mon-Sun with quality pre-loved apparel, shoes and accessories.
  Best for a reliable daily browse right on Flinders Street.
- **Lifeline Shop Townsville City** (289 Flinders St, Townsville City). A standard
  charity op shop, Mon-Sat trading. Best for pairing with Vinnies and Red Cross on
  the one CBD strip — all three sit within about 20 doors of each other.
- **SAHN Markets** (venue rotates around the CBD, most recently The Warehouse, 743
  Flinders St). A monthly pre-loved and vintage fashion market running since 2023,
  ticketed via TryBooking. Best for a curated vintage market day rather than a
  standing shopfront — check the current venue and date before visiting.

## West End

- **Vinnies Townsville** (1 Woodman Court, West End). Australia's biggest Vinnies
  store at 1,600m² (opened September 2023), with a dedicated formal and wedding
  section with fitting rooms plus an on-site book and gift wing. Best for the
  single biggest secondhand haul in the region, covering everyday fashion through
  to bridal.
- **RSPCA Op Shop Townsville** (1-3 Woodman Court, West End). A general thrift
  store — clothes, accessories, homewares, furniture — open seven days. Best for
  an animal-welfare-supporting stop right next to the big Vinnies.

## Garbutt and Domain Central

- **Vinnies Townsville Domain** (Shop G03A/103 Duckworth St, Garbutt). A standard
  charity op shop inside the Domain Central shopping centre. Best for combining a
  browse with a regular shopping-centre trip.
- **Salvos Stores Domain Central** (Shop G03B/103 Duckworth St, Garbutt). A Salvos
  family store in the same centre as Vinnies Domain. Best for pairing with Vinnies
  Domain in one stop.
- **Lifeline Superstore Garbutt** (233 Ingham Rd, Garbutt). A larger-format
  Lifeline outlet, Mon-Sat trading. Best for high-volume hunting across every
  category.
- **Revival Opp Shop** (37 Meenan St, Garbutt). A volunteer-run $2 op shop through
  Townsville Revival Fellowship. Best for rock-bottom prices on everyday basics.

## Belgian Gardens and North Ward

- **Vinnies Belgian Gardens** (Shop 5/49 Bundock St & Heatley Pde, Belgian
  Gardens). A standard charity op shop. Best for a charity browse closest to North
  Ward.

## Hermit Park and Hyde Park

Charters Towers Road carries a run of four charity shops through Hermit Park and
into Hyde Park.

- **Vinnies Hermit Park** (221 Charters Towers Rd, Hermit Park). A standard
  charity op shop. Best for an everyday browse on the Charters Towers Road strip.
- **Vinnies Hermit Park Gift & Book Shop** (219 Charters Towers Rd, Hermit Park,
  next door to the main shop above). A dedicated book and gift offshoot. Best for
  pairing with the main Hermit Park shop on the same visit.
- **Lifeline Shop Rising Sun** (260 Charters Towers Rd, Hermit Park). A standard
  charity op shop. Best for a third stop on the same Charters Towers Road run.
- **Salvos Stores Hyde Park** (36 Kings Rd, Hyde Park). A Salvos family store
  selling clothes, toys, books, furniture and household items. Best for
  high-volume hunting across every category.
- **Wounded Heroes OpShop** (Shop 20, 36-48 Kings Rd, Hyde Park, on the same small
  strip as the Salvos store above). A veteran-support charity shop open daily,
  selling pre-loved clothing and homewares. Best for a charity stop that directly
  funds crisis support for veterans and their families.

## Currajong

- **Lifeline Shop Currajong** (3/216 Woolcock St, Currajong). A standard charity
  op shop. Best for an everyday Woolcock Street browse.

## Aitkenvale and Kirwan

- **Lifeline Shop Aitkenvale** (276-280 Ross River Rd, Aitkenvale). A charity op
  shop on the main Ross River Road shopping strip. Best for an everyday browse in
  Aitkenvale.
- **Vinnies Kirwan** (Shop 1, 375 Charles St, Kirwan). A standard charity op shop.
  Best for a charity browse in Kirwan.
- **Lifeline Shop Kirwan** (Shop 2&4, 50 Bamford Lane, Kirwan). A charity op shop.
  Best for pairing with Vinnies Kirwan on the same trip.

## Idalia

- **Lifeline Shop Idalia** (10/1 Darcy Dr, Idalia). A standard charity op shop.
  Best for an everyday browse in Idalia.

## Deeragun and Thuringowa

- **Vinnies Deeragun** (Shop 3/12 Deeragun Rd, Deeragun). A charity op shop. Best
  for a northern-suburbs charity browse.
- **Salvos Stores Deeragun** (14 Deeragun Rd, Deeragun, on the same street as
  Vinnies Deeragun). A Salvos store. Best for pairing with Vinnies Deeragun in one
  stop.
- **Salvos Stores Willows** (2/45 Carthew St, Thuringowa Central). A Salvos family
  store. Best for high-volume hunting in Thuringowa Central.

## Magnetic Island

- **Vinnies Magnetic Island** (Shop 4-98/100 Sooning St, Nelly Bay). The island's
  own charity op shop. Best for a secondhand browse while island-hopping rather
  than driving back to the mainland.

## What to expect

Charity-shop prices dominate the Townsville scene — the Flinders Street and
Charters Towers Road strips are almost entirely Vinnies, Salvos, Red Cross and
Lifeline. SAHN Markets is the exception, a ticketed monthly event rather than a
daily browse, so it's worth checking the current date and venue before planning a
trip around it.

## Tips for finding deals

- **Start at the West End megastore.** Vinnies Townsville's 1,600m² floor is the
  biggest single haul in the region, with a dedicated formal and wedding section
  most charity shops don't have.
- **Do the Charters Towers Road run.** Four shops — two Vinnies, a Lifeline and a
  Wounded Heroes op shop — sit within a few minutes of each other through Hermit
  Park and Hyde Park.
- **Know your measurements.** Sizing varies hugely across brands and eras. Our
  [how to measure a garment guide](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and [brand size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help when an item has no fitting
  room.
