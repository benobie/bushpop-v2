# Help

Quick answers for buyers and sellers. If you can't find what you need, email support@bushpop.com.au and a real person in Australia will get back to you, usually within one business day.

## For buyers

### How do I track my order?

Your tracking number appears in your order confirmation email and on your order page once the seller posts your item. Sellers are expected to post within 3 business days of payment. If there's no tracking after that, message the seller first; if you don't hear back within 2 business days, contact support@bushpop.com.au and we'll chase it up.

### What if my item isn't as described?

You're covered by [Buyer Protection](/terms/buyer-protection/). Raise the issue within 7 days of delivery: contact the seller first, and if it isn't resolved, open a dispute with us. Full details, including what qualifies and what doesn't, are in our [Returns Policy](/terms/returns/).

### How do I contact a seller?

Use the message button on any listing or on your order page. {/* TODO: Ben confirm — messaging (same [CONFIRM: messaging] as above). */} Most sellers reply within a day or two. If a seller has gone quiet on an active order, contact support@bushpop.com.au.

## For sellers

### How do I list an item?

Listing is free. Photograph your item in good light, add the brand, size, condition and measurements, set your price and delivery options, and publish. The full walkthrough is in our [selling guide](/about/selling/).

### When do I get paid?

Your payment is held safely until the buyer confirms they've received the item, or automatically released 3 days after tracked delivery, whichever comes first.

### What happens if a buyer opens a dispute?

We'll email you straight away with what the buyer has raised. Reply within 2 business days with your side and any evidence (photos, tracking, the listing description). We look at both sides and work to a fair outcome; most disputes are resolved within a week.

## Contact us

Email support@bushpop.com.au or use the [contact page](/contact/). We aim to reply within one business day.
