# Sell Clothes Online Free

Turn the clothes you no longer wear into cash. The new Bushpop marketplace is
launching soon; here is how selling will work. Listing is completely free, your
buyers are local, and you post sold items straight to them. This is the quick
overview; for the full walkthrough see the
[complete selling guide](/selling/how-to-sell-on-bushpop-the-complete-guide/).

## Why sell on Bushpop

- **Free to list.** No listing fees, no monthly fees, and nothing charged until
  it sells.
- **Australian audience.** Reach buyers across Australia who are looking for
  preloved fashion, with prices and postage that work locally.
- **Simple and safe.** Payments and messaging run through Bushpop, so you can
  list in minutes and sell with confidence.

## How it works

1. **List it.** Snap a few photos, add measurements and set your price.
2. **Sell it.** A buyer purchases through Bushpop.
3. **Ship it.** Post the item with tracking, ideally within two business days.
4. **Get paid.** We deduct 1.75% + 30¢ before your payout lands, and the rest
   is yours.

## What it costs

Listing is free. When an item sells, Bushpop takes 1.75% + 30¢ from your
payout, and that is the only seller fee. Buyers separately pay a Buyer
Protection fee at checkout, so it never comes out of your payout. Compared
with marketplaces that charge sellers around 10% or more, Bushpop's seller
fee is a fraction of that.
