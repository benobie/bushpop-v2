# Sell Clothes Online Free

Turn the clothes you no longer wear into cash. Listing on Bushpop is completely
free, your buyers are local, and you post sold items straight to them. This is the
quick overview; for the full walkthrough see the
[complete selling guide](/selling/how-to-sell-on-bushpop-the-complete-guide/).

## Why sell on Bushpop

- **Free to sell.** No listing fees, no monthly fees, and you keep your full asking
  price. The buyer pays the Bushpop fee, not you.
- **Australian audience.** Reach buyers across Australia who are looking for
  preloved fashion, with prices and postage that work locally.
- **Simple and safe.** We handle secure payments and messaging, so you can list in
  minutes and sell with confidence.

## How it works

1. **List it.** Snap a few photos, add measurements and set your price.
2. **Sell it.** A buyer purchases and their payment is held securely.
3. **Ship it.** Post the item with tracking, usually within two business days.
4. **Get paid.** Funds are released to you once the buyer receives the item.

## What it costs

Selling is free. Buyers pay a small Buyer Protection fee at checkout that covers
secure payment and support. Compared with marketplaces that charge sellers around
10% or more, Bushpop lets you keep the full value of your sale.
