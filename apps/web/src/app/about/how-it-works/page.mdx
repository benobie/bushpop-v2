# How Bushpop Works

Bushpop is a peer-to-peer marketplace for secondhand fashion. Sellers list their
own items and post them directly to buyers. Bushpop provides the marketplace,
secure payments and messaging that make each sale simple and safe.

## The basics

Every item on Bushpop comes from a real person clearing out their wardrobe.
Sellers create listings with photos, measurements and a price. Buyers browse,
ask questions and purchase. Once an item sells, the seller posts it straight to
the buyer and Bushpop releases the payment. We facilitate the transaction; we
never take possession of the item.

## For buyers

1. **Browse** thousands of preloved pieces from sellers across Australia.
2. **Check the details** — photos, measurements, condition and the seller's
   ratings. Message the seller with any questions before you buy.
3. **Buy securely** through Bushpop. A small Buyer Protection fee applies at
   checkout and your payment is protected while the order is completed.
4. **Receive your item** posted directly by the seller, with tracking.

New to buying preloved? Our [buying guide](/about/buying/) covers sizing,
condition and getting the best finds.

## For sellers

1. **List your item** with a few photos, measurements and a price. Listing is
   free.
2. **Make the sale** when a buyer purchases.
3. **Ship it** within a couple of business days, with tracking.
4. **Get paid** once the buyer receives the item.

Our [selling guide](/about/selling/) walks through photos, pricing, postage and
getting paid in detail.

## Trust and safety

Payments are held securely until an order is completed, so buyers are protected if
something goes wrong and sellers know the funds are there before they post. Seller
ratings build over time so the community can buy with confidence, and our support
team is here if a dispute needs resolving.

## Fees

**Selling is free.** Listing costs nothing, there are no monthly fees and there is
no charge for items that do not sell. Sellers keep their full asking price.

Buyers pay a **Buyer Protection fee** of 7% (with a small minimum) on top of the
item price at checkout. It is shown clearly before you pay and it covers secure
payment, support and help resolving any issue with your order.
