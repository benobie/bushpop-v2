# How Bushpop Works

Bushpop is a peer-to-peer marketplace for secondhand fashion. The new
marketplace is launching soon; here is how buying and selling will work.
Sellers list their own items and post them directly to buyers, and Bushpop
provides the marketplace, payments and messaging that make each sale simple
and safe.

## The basics

Every item on Bushpop comes from a real person clearing out their wardrobe.
Sellers create listings with photos, measurements and a price. Buyers browse,
ask questions and purchase. Once an item sells, the seller posts it straight to
the buyer. We facilitate the transaction; we never take possession of the item.

## For buyers

1. **Browse** preloved pieces from sellers across Australia.
2. **Check the details**: photos, measurements and condition. Message the
   seller with any questions before you buy.
3. **Buy with protection.** A Buyer Protection fee applies at checkout on
   posted orders (free for local pickup), so every purchase is covered.
4. **Receive your item** posted directly by the seller, with tracking.

New to buying preloved? Our [buying guide](/about/buying/) covers sizing,
condition and getting the best finds.

## For sellers

1. **List your item** with a few photos, measurements and a price. Listing is
   free.
2. **Make the sale** when a buyer purchases.
3. **Ship it** within a couple of business days, with tracking.
4. **Get paid.** We deduct 1.75% + 30¢ before your payout lands, and the rest
   is yours.

Our [selling guide](/about/selling/) walks through photos, pricing, postage and
getting paid in detail.

## Trust and safety

Every order is covered by Buyer Protection, so buyers are protected if something
goes wrong. Seller reviews are coming with the marketplace launch, so the
community can buy with confidence as reputations build. If a dispute needs
resolving, human support, based in Australia, is there to help.

## Fees

**Listing is free.** There are no listing fees and no monthly fees. When an
item sells, Bushpop takes 1.75% + 30¢ from the seller's payout, and that is
the only seller fee.

Buyers pay a **Buyer Protection fee** of 4% of the item price plus 50¢ on
posted orders, on top of the item price at checkout, with no cap. Local
pickup orders pay no Buyer Protection fee at all. The fee is shown clearly
before you pay and it covers payment processing, support and help resolving
any issue with your order.
