# Australia's Online Op Shop

Bushpop is like an op shop you can browse from your couch, with sellers all over
Australia. The new marketplace is launching soon; here is how buying will work.
Find preloved, vintage and designer fashion at a fraction of retail, and buy with
confidence knowing every order is covered by Buyer Protection.

## Why buy on Bushpop

- **Better prices.** Quality brands cost far less secondhand than new.
- **More sustainable.** Every preloved purchase keeps clothing in use and out of
  landfill.
- **Unique finds.** Vintage, one-off and sold-out pieces you will not find in a
  store, all from real Australian wardrobes.

## Browsing and search

When the marketplace launches you will be able to search by keyword, browse by
category and filter by **size, brand, condition and price** to narrow things
down fast.

## Making a purchase

When you find something you love, check the photos and measurements, and message
the seller with any questions. When you are ready, buy through Bushpop. Every
order is covered by Buyer Protection, and the small Buyer Protection fee is
shown clearly at checkout.

## Payment methods

Checkout will take major cards when the marketplace launches, with the exact
payment options confirmed then. You will always see the full price, including
the Buyer Protection fee, before you confirm your order.

## Shipping and delivery

Sellers post items directly to you, with tracking so you can follow your parcel,
and are asked to post within two business days. Postage is set by the seller and
shown on the listing, so you know the full cost before you buy.

## What if something goes wrong

If an item does not arrive, or it is not as described, you are covered. Message the
seller first, since most issues are resolved quickly that way. If you cannot reach
a resolution, [contact support](/contact/) and a real person, based in Australia,
will help under Buyer Protection. See our [returns policy](/terms/returns/) for
the details.

## Buyer tips

- **Read the listing closely.** Condition notes and flaw photos tell you exactly
  what you are getting.
- **Ask questions** before you buy: measurements, fit and condition.
- **Use the [size charts](/guides/size-charts/)** to match an item to your
  measurements, especially for vintage and international brands. The same guide
  explains [how to measure a garment](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and what each [condition grade](/guides/size-charts/#condition-guide) means.
