# Australia's Online Op Shop

Bushpop is like an op shop you can browse from your couch, stocked by sellers all
over Australia. Find preloved, vintage and designer fashion at a fraction of retail,
and buy with confidence knowing every purchase is covered by Buyer Protection.

## Why buy on Bushpop

- **Better prices.** Quality brands cost far less secondhand than new.
- **More sustainable.** Every preloved purchase keeps clothing in use and out of
  landfill.
- **Unique finds.** Vintage, one-off and sold-out pieces you will not find in a
  store, all from real Australian wardrobes.

## Browsing and search

Search by keyword, or browse by category. Filter by **size, brand, condition and
price** to narrow things down fast. Found a seller you like? Follow them to see
their new listings first.

## Making a purchase

When you find something you love, check the photos, measurements and the seller's
ratings, and message the seller with any questions. When you are ready, buy
securely through Bushpop. Your payment is held safely until your order is
completed, and a small Buyer Protection fee is shown clearly at checkout.

## Payment methods

Bushpop accepts major cards and common online payment methods. You always see the
full price, including the Buyer Protection fee, before you confirm your order.

## Shipping and delivery

Sellers post items directly to you, usually within a couple of business days, with
tracking so you can follow your parcel. Postage is set by the seller and shown on
the listing, so you know the full cost before you buy.

## What if something goes wrong

If an item does not arrive, or it is not as described, you are covered. Message the
seller first, since most issues are resolved quickly that way. If you cannot reach
a resolution, [contact our support team](/contact/) and we will help under Buyer
Protection. See our [returns policy](/terms/returns/) for the details.

## Buyer tips

- **Check the seller's ratings** and read recent reviews.
- **Ask questions** before you buy — measurements, fit and condition.
- **Use the [size charts](/guides/size-charts/)** to match an item to your
  measurements, especially for vintage and international brands. The same guide
  explains [how to measure a garment](/guides/size-charts/#how-to-measure-a-garment)
  and what each [condition grade](/guides/size-charts/#condition-guide) means.
