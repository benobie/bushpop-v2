# How to Sell on Bushpop — The Complete Guide

Selling on Bushpop is free and takes minutes to start. This guide covers
everything: setting up your shop, photographing and pricing your items, posting a
sale, getting paid and building a reputation that brings buyers back. If you just
want the quick version, see the [sell overview](/about/selling/).

## Getting started

Create a free account and set up your seller profile. Your profile is your shop,
so add a clear profile photo, a short bio and your general location. Verify your
email so buyers know you are a real seller, then connect a payment method so your
payouts can be released after a sale. That is it: there are no listing fees and no
subscription.

## What sells well on Bushpop

Bushpop buyers are looking for preloved fashion at fair prices. The items that move
fastest are:

- **In-demand Australian and global brands** such as Gorman, Country Road,
  Lululemon, Zara and Nike.
- **Good-condition everyday pieces** — denim, knitwear, dresses, jackets and
  quality basics.
- **Vintage, designer and one-of-a-kind finds** that are hard to get new.

Honest condition matters more than perfection. Clean, well-described items in
"good" or "very good" condition sell reliably when they are priced sensibly.

## Creating a listing that sells

A strong listing answers every question a buyer has before they need to ask. The
three things that sell an item are clear photos, an accurate description and the
right price.

### Photography

Photos do most of the selling, so it is worth getting them right:

- Shoot in **natural light** — outdoors or next to a window, never under harsh
  overhead lights.
- Use a **clean, plain background** so the item stands out.
- Show the item **flat-laid or on a body or hanger**, then add detail shots of the
  brand tag, fabric texture and any flaws.
- Include **at least three photos**. More is better, especially the back, the
  label and any wear.

### Writing your title

Put the most important details first so the listing is easy to find. Include the
**brand, item type, colour and size**. For example: "Gorman leopard print blouse,
size 10".

### Writing your description

Describe the **condition honestly** using clear language, and disclose any flaws
up front. Include the **brand, fabric and any fit notes**, and always add
**measurements** (see below). Buyers trust sellers who are upfront, and accurate
listings mean fewer issues after the sale.

### Sizing and measurements

Always include **actual measurements** taken flat: bust or chest, waist and
length. Sizes vary a lot between brands and eras, so measurements are the single
most useful thing you can give a buyer. Link the relevant
[brand size chart](/guides/size-charts/) in your description, especially for
vintage and international labels, to help buyers choose with confidence.

## Pricing your items

Price against the real secondhand market, not the original retail price. A useful
starting point is the item's **RRP adjusted for condition and demand**: a near-new
piece from a sought-after brand holds more of its value than a well-worn basic.
Search Bushpop and other marketplaces for what comparable items actually sold for.
Price fairly, but do not undervalue good pieces — you can always consider offers.

## Shipping

You post sold items directly to the buyer. Keep it simple and reliable:

- Use **Australia Post** (Parcel Post or Express) or a service like **Sendle**.
- Package items so they arrive clean and undamaged; reused packaging is fine.
- **Always use tracking** so both you and the buyer can follow the parcel.
- **Postage is usually paid by the buyer** — set your postage price in the listing
  so there are no surprises at checkout.
- Aim to **post within two business days** of a sale.

## Getting paid

When a buyer purchases, their payment is held securely by Bushpop. Once you post
the item and the buyer receives it, the funds are **released to your connected
payment method**. Selling is free, so there is nothing deducted from your sale; the
buyer pays a small Buyer Protection fee at checkout, not you.

## Building your seller reputation

Repeat buyers come from good experiences. Reply to questions quickly, describe
items accurately, post promptly and pack with care. Positive ratings build over
time and make every future listing easier to sell. Staying active and listing
regularly also keeps your shop visible to followers.

## Seller FAQs

**What can I sell?** Preloved, vintage, upcycled or new clothing, shoes and
accessories, across menswear, womenswear and kids. No counterfeits or prohibited
items.

**Is it free to list?** Yes. Listing and selling are free, with no monthly fees and
no charge for items that do not sell.

**What are Bushpop's fees for sellers?** None. The buyer pays a Buyer Protection
fee at checkout; you keep your full asking price.

**What if a buyer says an item is not as described?** Honest, detailed listings
prevent most disputes. If an issue arises, our support team helps resolve it
fairly under Buyer Protection. Accurate photos and measurements are your best
protection.
