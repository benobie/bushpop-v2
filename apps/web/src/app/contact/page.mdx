# Contact Bushpop

We are an Australian team and we are happy to help. Before you email, our
[help centre](/help/) answers the most common questions about buying, selling,
postage and payments.

## Get in touch

The fastest way to reach us is by email at **support@bushpop.com.au**. We aim to
reply within one business day.

## For order support

If you have an issue with an order, message the other party through Bushpop first;
most questions about postage or an item are sorted quickly that way. If you cannot
reach a resolution, email **support@bushpop.com.au** with your order number and we
will step in.

## For seller enquiries

Questions about your account, listings or payouts? Email
**support@bushpop.com.au** and the team will help you get set up or sort out any
issue.

## For media and press

For media, press or partnership enquiries, email **support@bushpop.com.au**.

## For legal and privacy

For legal, privacy or data requests, email **support@bushpop.com.au**. See our
[privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) for how we handle your information.
