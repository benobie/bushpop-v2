# Best Vintage Markets on the Gold Coast

The Gold Coast's preloved scene has gone from strength to strength, anchored by a
thriving vintage market and the city's long-running beachfront and craft markets.
If you love thrifting in person, here are the markets worth planning a weekend
around.

## Markets to visit

### Gold Coast Vintage Market

The Gold Coast's biggest preloved fashion and vinyl event, held at the
**Broadbeach Cultural Centre** every couple of months. Expect hundreds of racks of
curated vintage, Y2K and streetwear plus crates of records from Queensland
collectors, with food trucks and DJs on site. A small entry fee gets you in early
for the best picks. Dates change through the year, so check
**@goldcoastvintagemarket** on Instagram for the next one.

### Surfers Paradise Beachfront Markets

A free seaside night market along the **Surfers Paradise Esplanade**, running
multiple evenings a week. It is more of a mixed market than pure vintage, but it is
a great atmosphere for a browse with live music and the beach right there. Check
current days and times before you go.

### Gold Coast Art and Craft Markets

The originals: a long-running set of beachfront markets across **Broadbeach,
Coolangatta, Burleigh Heads and Paradise Point**, on rotating weekends each month.
Alongside art, craft and food you will often find preloved and handmade fashion.
Confirm the current schedule for each location before heading down.

## What to expect

Markets are cash-and-card, but bring some cash for smaller stalls. Prices range
from a few dollars for basics up to premium for curated vintage and designer
pieces. Mornings are best for the freshest stock; the vintage market in particular
rewards arriving early.

## Tips for market shopping

- **Arrive early** for the best selection, especially at the vintage market.
- **Bring your measurements** — market pieces rarely have a fitting room, and
  vintage sizing runs differently to modern labels (our
  [size charts](/guides/size-charts/) help).
- **Carry some cash** for stalls that prefer it, and a tote so you are not juggling
  bags.
