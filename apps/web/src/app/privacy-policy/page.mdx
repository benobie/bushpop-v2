We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## 2. What information we collect

We collect the information we need to run a marketplace, and not much more:

- **Account information.** Your name, email address, and password when you create an account, plus your delivery address when you buy and your payout details when you sell.
- **Transaction information.** Orders you place, items you sell, prices, fees, shipping details, and tracking numbers.
- **Listing content.** Photos, descriptions, measurements, and condition notes you add to listings.
- **Messages and support.** Messages you exchange through the platform and emails you send to support@bushpop.com.au.
- **Payment information.** Payments are processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store your full card number on our systems.
- **Technical and analytics data.** Device and browser information, IP address, pages visited, and interactions with the site, collected via cookies and analytics tools (see section 6).

## 3. How we collect it

- **Directly from you**, when you create an account, list an item, buy something, or contact support.
- **Automatically**, as you use the site, through cookies, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), and Google Tag Manager (GTM).
- **From other users**, in the course of a transaction. For example, when you buy an item, the seller receives the details needed to post it to you.

## 4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- operate the marketplace: accounts, listings, checkout, orders, and payouts;
- process payments and pass delivery details to sellers and shipping providers;
- send service communications such as order confirmations, shipping updates, and dispute correspondence;
- provide human support when something goes wrong;
- keep the platform safe: detecting fraud, enforcing our [Terms and Conditions](/terms/), and handling Buyer Protection claims;
- understand how the site is used, via analytics, so we can improve it; and
- meet our legal obligations, including tax and record-keeping requirements.

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to running Bushpop, and we do not sell it to anyone.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed to provide the service:

- **Payment processors**, to process payments and seller payouts.
- **Shipping providers**, where shipping labels or tracking are arranged through the platform.
- **Other users**, to the extent needed to complete a transaction (for example, a seller sees the buyer's delivery details).
- **Service providers** that host our infrastructure, deliver our emails, and provide analytics (including Google, for GA4 and GTM).
- **Professional advisers and authorities**, where the law requires it or where it is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

Some of our service providers store data outside Australia (for example, in the United States). ## 6. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and measure how the site is used. Analytics is provided by Google Analytics 4, deployed via Google Tag Manager. Analytics data is used in aggregate to understand site usage, not to identify you personally.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser opt-out tools. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working.

## 7. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act you can:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** it if it is inaccurate, out of date, or incomplete.
- **Ask us to delete** your account and associated personal information. We will do so, except where we are required to keep records (for example, transaction records for tax purposes).

To exercise any of these rights, email support@bushpop.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable period, and we will not charge you for making a request.

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first and we will work to put it right. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au/).

## 8. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. That includes encryption in transit, access controls on our systems, and using a payment processor so that card details never touch our servers.

If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected users and the OAIC in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

## 9. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active, and afterwards only as long as we need it to meet legal obligations (such as tax and transaction record-keeping) or to resolve disputes. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

## 10. Children's privacy

Bushpop is not directed at people under 18, and you must be 18 or older to hold an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact support@bushpop.com.au and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be at this page, with the date above updated. If we make a material change to how we handle your personal information, we will let account holders know by email before it takes effect.

## 12. Contact

Questions, access or correction requests, and privacy complaints all go to the same place: email support@bushpop.com.au or use our [contact page](/contact/). A real person in Australia reads and answers. If we cannot resolve a complaint, you can escalate it to the OAIC (see section 7).
