# Shipping Policy

*Last updated: 8 July 2026*

## How shipping works

Bushpop is a peer-to-peer marketplace: sellers ship their items directly to buyers. Each listing shows the delivery options the seller offers. Where a shipping price is set, buyers pay it at checkout on top of the item price; some sellers include postage in the item price instead. Sellers are expected to post within 3 business days of confirmed payment.

## Shipping options

Sellers arrange their own postage and can use any reputable tracked service, such as Australia Post or Sendle. The options and prices you see at checkout are set by the seller on each listing.

Some listings offer local pickup. If you choose pickup at checkout, arrange a time and public place with the seller through messages. It's similar to a posted order: instant payout to the seller and narrower cover, so inspect the item before you redeem your collection code, but there's no Buyer Protection fee on pickup.

## Tracking

Sellers must provide tracking whenever the service they use offers it. You'll find your tracking number in your order confirmation email and on your order page. If no tracking has been provided within 3 business days of payment, message the seller, then contact support@bushpop.com.au if you don't hear back.

## Estimated delivery times

As a rough guide once posted: 2 to 6 business days within metro areas, and longer for regional addresses, WA and NT. These are estimates based on carrier guidance, not guarantees.

## International shipping

Bushpop is an Australian marketplace and most listings ship within Australia only. Some sellers may offer international postage on individual listings; where they do, delivery times vary widely and any customs charges or import duties are the buyer's responsibility.

## What if my order doesn't arrive

Check the tracking first, then message the seller. If the seller doesn't respond within 2 business days, or the item has clearly gone missing, open a dispute; non-delivery is covered by [Buyer Protection](/terms/buyer-protection/). See the [Returns Policy](/terms/returns/) for how disputes work.

## Seller shipping responsibilities

Sellers must pack items securely and weatherproof (a compostable satchel or reused mailer is fine, wet cardboard is not), post within 3 business days, and add tracking where available. If an item is damaged in transit, the buyer is covered by Buyer Protection; we strongly recommend sellers use tracked and, for higher-value items, insured services.
