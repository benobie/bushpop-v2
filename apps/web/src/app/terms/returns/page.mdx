## Overview

Everything on Bushpop is one-of-one, so returns work differently from a retail store: there is no shelf of identical replacements to swap yours for. Bushpop is a peer-to-peer marketplace, which means there are two paths, and it helps to know which one you are on.

**Something's wrong with the order.** It never arrived, it arrived damaged, or it is significantly not as described. That is not a return in the retail sense; it is a [Buyer Protection](/terms/buyer-protection/) claim, and it is covered on every order paid through Bushpop checkout.

**Change of mind, or fit.** The marketplace default: change-of-mind returns are between you and the seller. ## When you can return an item

A Buyer Protection claim covers:

- your item never arrives;
- your item arrives damaged; or
- your item is significantly not as described: the wrong item, the wrong size against the listed measurements, or condition clearly below the listed grade.

What does not qualify: change of mind, or fit issues where the listing's measurements were accurate. Minor wear consistent with the listed condition grade is not a defect; that is what the five-step condition scale is for.

## Seller obligations

When a claim is raised on one of your orders, we will email you straight away with what the buyer has raised. Reply within 2 business days with your side and any evidence: photos, tracking, the listing description. We look at both sides and work to a fair outcome; most disputes are resolved within a week.

If the resolution is a return-for-refund, the payout for that order stays paused until tracking confirms the item is back with you.

## Dodge the dud fit before you buy

Most "it doesn't fit" moments never need to happen, because every listing carries the two things a size label can't give you:

- **Real measurements.** Compare them against a piece you already own and love, not against a brand's idea of a size.
- **The five-step condition scale.** New with tags · Like new · Excellent · Good · Well loved, so "good" means the same thing on every listing.

Buying a brand you don't know? Check our [size charts](/guides/size-charts/) before you buy; we keep charts for the brands Australians search most.

## Australian Consumer Law

This policy sits alongside your rights under the Australian Consumer Law; it does not replace or limit them. Consumer guarantees apply to goods sold in trade or commerce and cannot be excluded by these terms. Secondhand items are judged against their age, price, and disclosed condition, but you are always entitled to a remedy if an item is not as described.

If we cannot resolve an issue and you believe your consumer rights have not been met, you can contact the ACCC or your state or territory fair trading body.
