# Buyer Protection

*Draft · 8 July 2026*

> **Draft policy, pending review.** This is a working draft. It becomes our binding policy once it has been reviewed by our lawyer and ratified; until then it is a draft, not a promise.

## How it works

Every order paid through Bushpop checkout is covered. That is possible because of how the money moves: when you pay, your money sits with Bushpop, not the seller, until you confirm you have received the item or until 3 days after tracked delivery, whichever comes first. If something goes wrong, the money is still here to put it right.

## What it costs

Buyer Protection is one visible line at checkout, shown before you pay: **4% of the item price + 50¢** on posted orders. On **local pickup it's $0**, and cover is still included on every order either way; it is never opt-in and never fee-gated. The fee is what funds the holding of your payment, the claims work, and the refund when something goes wrong.

## What's covered

- Your item never arrives.
- Your item arrives damaged.
- Your item is significantly not as described: the wrong item, the wrong size against the listed measurements, or condition clearly below the listed grade.
- The item turns out to be counterfeit. Counterfeit items are banned on Bushpop; if you receive one, it is covered.

## What's not covered

- Change of mind, or fit. See our [Returns Policy](/terms/returns/) for how those work.
- Minor wear consistent with the listed condition grade; that is what the five-step scale is for.
- Anything bought or paid for off the platform. Cover only exists inside Bushpop checkout.

Pickup orders are confirmed with a collection code. Once you redeem the code, the sale completes and the seller's payout releases on the spot, so check the item before you redeem it. ## Sellers

Protection runs both ways, with one hard rule: no tracking, no seller protection. Post without a tracking number and your parcel is not covered. Our prepaid labels are tracked by default.

## Your consumer rights

Buyer Protection sits alongside your rights under the Australian Consumer Law; it does not replace or limit them. See our [Returns Policy](/terms/returns/) for more on how the two fit together.
